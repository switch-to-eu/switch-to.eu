## Current state
Startpage says it is owned by a Dutch company headquartered in The Hague and is part of System1, a publicly traded US company. [[2026-11-03-startpage-what-is-startpage#c2]] System1 lists Surfboard Holding B.V. (Netherlands) as a subsidiary. [[2026-11-03-system1-10k-2022-subsidiaries#c2]] The size of System1's stake has not been disclosed. [[2026-11-03-computing-startpage-system1-investment#c2]]
