## Current state
Ecosia says its results and ads come from Microsoft Bing, Google and EUSP, with EUSP results served only in France. [[2026-11-03-ecosia-search-results-providers#c1]] [[2026-11-03-ecosia-search-results-providers#c3]] It co-owns EUSP 50/50 with Qwant. [[2026-11-03-qwant-ecosia-eusp-announcement#c1]] It shares the user's IP address and search terms with Bing or Google and anonymises IP addresses after at most 7 days. ## Facts
- 2026-11-03 · As of 2026-11-03, says its results and related ads come from Microsoft Bing, Google and EUSP. Bing results are available to all users, Google results only in specific regions and browsers, and EUSP results only in France. [[2026-11-03-ecosia-search-results-providers#c1]] [[2026-11-03-ecosia-search-results-providers#c3]]
- 2026-05-21 · As of its privacy policy of 2026-05-21, shares the user's IP address and search terms automatically with Microsoft Bing or Google, and anonymises IP addresses after at most 7 days. [[2026-11-03-ecosia-privacy-policy#c2]] [[2026-11-03-ecosia-privacy-policy#c3]]
- 2025-08-06 · Started serving search queries through Staan, the index of its joint venture EUSP with Qwant. [[2026-11-03-techcrunch-staan-launch#c1]] [[2026-11-03-techcrunch-staan-launch#c4]]
- 2025-07-14 · As of 2025-07-14, describes itself as a not-for-profit social business that puts 100% of its profits into climate action, earning money from ad clicks through Google AdSense and the Microsoft Advertising network. [[2026-11-03-ecosia-how-it-makes-money#c1]] [[2026-11-03-ecosia-how-it-makes-money#c2]]
- 2024-11-08 · Announced European Search Perspective (EUSP), a joint venture based in Paris and owned 50/50 by Ecosia and Qwant, set up outside Ecosia's steward-owned model so it can raise outside capital.
