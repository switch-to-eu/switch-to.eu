## Current state
Nextcloud is based in Stuttgart and led by founder Frank Karlitschek. [[2026-11-03-heise-nextcloud-ionos-school-platform#c3]] [[2026-11-03-heise-nextcloud-ionos-school-platform#c8]] Its software is the basis of IONOS Nextcloud Workspace, a Microsoft 365 alternative with file sharing, chat, video calls, email, calendar and office apps. [[2026-11-03-heise-nextcloud-ionos-school-platform#c4]] Since November 2026 schools in six German states can use that workspace under a four-year framework contract won by IONOS.
