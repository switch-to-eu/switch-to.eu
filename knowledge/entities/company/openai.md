## Current state
OpenAI plans to retire custom GPTs in ChatGPT on 2026-12-11 and move creators to plugins. [[2026-11-03-openai-gpts-help#c1]] [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-retirement-faq#c1]] New GPT creation and publishing are not available on personal ChatGPT accounts. [[2026-11-03-openai-gpts-help#c2]] Migration turns a GPT's instructions into a skill and copies its knowledge files and connected apps into the plugin. Drafts, the selected model and custom actions do not transfer.
