## Current state
Mistral AI is a French company registered in Paris under number 952 418 325 and the data controller for consumer use of its products. [[2026-11-03-mistral-privacy-policy#c1]] Its consumer assistant Le Chat was renamed Vibe in May 2026. [[2026-11-03-mistral-vibe-agent-launch#c1]] Vibe input and output are used to train its models by default unless the user opts out. [[2026-11-03-mistral-help-training-data-use#c1]] Its subprocessor list names US-based providers for image generation and web search, and Microsoft and Google among its cloud infrastructure providers.
