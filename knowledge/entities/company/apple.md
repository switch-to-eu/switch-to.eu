## Current state
The Commission's 2026 DMA review lists Apple's iOS, iPadOS and Safari among the services that must let users change defaults easily. [[2026-11-03-ec-dma-review-2026-choice-screens#c1]] [[2026-11-03-ec-dma-review-2026-choice-screens#c2]] In the EU, iOS and iPadOS show a choice screen for the default browser. [[2026-11-03-apple-eu-browser-choice-screen#c1]] Its support page describes a Safari search engine choice screen for Japan, and its EU developer page describes no search engine choice screen. [[2026-11-03-apple-default-apps-choice-screens#c3]] [[2026-11-03-apple-eu-browser-choice-screen#c6]]

## Facts
- 2026-11-03 · As of 2026-11-03, its developer page says the EU browser choice screen shows up to 11 of the most downloaded eligible browsers per country in addition to Safari, in random order, with the list updated once a year. The page describes no search engine choice screen. [[2026-11-03-apple-eu-browser-choice-screen#c4]] [[2026-11-03-apple-eu-browser-choice-screen#c6]]
- 2026-04-28 · The Commission's DMA review lists Apple's iOS, iPadOS and Safari under Article 6(3). [[2026-11-03-ec-dma-review-2026-choice-screens#c2]]
- 2025-12-18 · As of 2025-12-18, its support page says users in EU countries and Japan may be prompted to pick a default browser from a choice screen, and users in Japan may also be prompted to pick a default search engine in Safari. [[2026-11-03-apple-default-apps-choice-screens#c2]] [[2026-11-03-apple-default-apps-choice-screens#c3]]
- 2024-03-25 · The Commission opened a DMA non-compliance investigation into Apple's choice screen design. It closed the case after Apple made changes (closing date not in the source). [[2026-11-03-ec-dma-review-2026-choice-screens#c5]]
- 2024-02-12 · The Commission decided that iMessage should not be designated as a gatekeeper service. [[2026-11-03-ec-bing-edge-not-gatekeepers#c1]]
- 2023-09-05 · Designated a gatekeeper under the DMA from this date, per the Commission. [[2026-11-03-ec-bing-edge-not-gatekeepers#c3]]
