## Current state
Qwant says it partners with Microsoft for search results it cannot answer itself and for contextual ads. [[2026-11-03-qwant-privacy-policy#c2]] It co-owns European Search Perspective (EUSP) 50/50 with Ecosia and has served queries through EUSP's Staan index since August 2025. [[2026-11-03-qwant-ecosia-eusp-announcement#c1]] [[2026-11-03-techcrunch-staan-launch#c1]] Its privacy policy lists Mistral AI, with EUSP, as a partner for AI summaries. [[2026-11-03-qwant-privacy-policy#c6]]

## Facts
- 2026-11-03 · As of 2026-11-03, its privacy policy says it partners with Microsoft for search results "when we do not ourselves have the answers" and for contextual ads, and lists "European Perspective (Staan)" for processing queries and Mistral AI with European Perspective for AI summaries. [[2026-11-03-qwant-privacy-policy#c2]] [[2026-11-03-qwant-privacy-policy#c6]]
- 2026-11-03 · As of 2026-11-03, says it may send Microsoft the search keywords, browser information, the first three bytes of the IP address, an approximate region or city, a salted hash and a random token, and the full IP address for security purposes such as click-fraud detection. [[2026-11-03-qwant-privacy-policy#c3]] [[2026-11-03-qwant-privacy-policy#c4]]
- 2025-08-06 · Started serving search queries through Staan, and said it was using the index to power some features such as AI summaries. [[2026-11-03-techcrunch-staan-launch#c1]] [[2026-11-03-techcrunch-staan-launch#c3]]
- 2024-11-08 · Announced European Search Perspective (EUSP), a joint venture based in Paris and owned 50/50 by Ecosia and Qwant. [[2026-11-03-qwant-ecosia-eusp-announcement#c1]]
