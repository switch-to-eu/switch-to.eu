## Current state
European Search Perspective (EUSP) is based in Paris and owned 50/50 by Ecosia and Qwant. [[2026-11-03-qwant-ecosia-eusp-announcement#c1]] Its index, Staan, has served queries for both since August 2025. [[2026-11-03-techcrunch-staan-launch#c1]] [[2026-11-03-techcrunch-staan-launch#c4]] Ecosia says EUSP results are served only in France. [[2026-11-03-ecosia-search-results-providers#c3]] Qwant lists it as a partner for processing queries and, with Mistral AI, for AI summaries. [[2026-11-03-qwant-privacy-policy#c6]]

## Facts
- 2026-11-03 · As of 2026-11-03, Ecosia says EUSP results are served only in France. [[2026-11-03-ecosia-search-results-providers#c3]]
- 2026-11-03 · As of 2026-11-03, Qwant's privacy policy lists "European Perspective (Staan)" as a partner for processing queries, and Mistral AI with European Perspective for AI summaries. [[2026-11-03-qwant-privacy-policy#c6]]
- 2025-08-06 · Qwant and Ecosia started serving search queries through Staan. EUSP aimed to serve around 50% of French and 33% of German queries by the end of 2025, and was pitching Staan to companies, including AI chatbot makers, as a cheaper alternative to Google's and Bing's search APIs. It planned to serve both companies' France-based traffic by Q1 2025.
