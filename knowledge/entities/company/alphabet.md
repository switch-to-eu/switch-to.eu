## Current state
The Commission's 2026 DMA review lists Alphabet's Android, Chrome and Google Search among the services that must let users change defaults easily, including through choice screens. [[2026-11-03-ec-dma-review-2026-choice-screens#c1]] [[2026-11-03-ec-dma-review-2026-choice-screens#c2]] Each Google Account includes up to 15 GB of storage shared across Gmail, Google Drive and Google Photos. [[2026-11-03-google-storage-quota-help#c1]] Google Takeout archives expire after about 7 days and can be downloaded at most 5 times. [[2026-11-03-google-takeout-download-data-help#c6]]
