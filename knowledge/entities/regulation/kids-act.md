## Current state
The Commission proposed the Kids Act in September 2026. Politico says it would bar social media access for most under-13s, severely restrict access for under-15s and set safety-by-design rules, including for video games and AI companions. [[2026-11-03-politico-kids-act-national-bans-paused#c4]] [[2026-11-03-politico-kids-act-national-bans-paused#c5]] Member states, the European Parliament and the Commission still have to agree on it. [[2026-11-03-politico-kids-act-national-bans-paused#c6]] Meanwhile the Commission has told member states to pause overlapping national laws for 12 months. [[2026-11-03-politico-kids-act-national-bans-paused#c1]]
