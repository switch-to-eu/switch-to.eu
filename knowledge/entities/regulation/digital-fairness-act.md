## Current state
The Digital Fairness Act is the EU's planned revamp of online consumer law. Rapporteur reports the European Commission is due to present the proposal on 11 November 2026. [[2026-11-03-euractiv-dfa-social-media-switching#c5]] A coalition of alternative social app builders wants it to create a right to take your social network with you when you switch platforms, through a common "personal data server" standard for every account. [[2026-11-03-dfom-joint-position-paper#c4]] [[2026-11-03-dfom-joint-position-paper#c5]] The Unfair Commercial Practices Directive is widely expected to be changed alongside it. [[2026-11-03-euractiv-dfa-social-media-switching#c7]]

## Facts
- 2026-11-03 · More than 35 social app builders urged the Commission to use the DFA to give users a right to take their social network with them when they switch platforms.
