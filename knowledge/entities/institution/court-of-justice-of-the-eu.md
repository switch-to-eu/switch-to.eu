## Current state
In the first case about how far AI developers may go in using data, Advocate General Maciej Szpunar advised on 2026-11-03 that AI training on copyrighted works is as a rule allowed, but that chatbots can infringe the press publisher's right. The court has not ruled yet. [[2026-11-03-euractiv-ag-opinion-ai-news-reuse#c1]] [[2026-11-03-euractiv-ag-opinion-ai-news-reuse#c2]] [[2026-11-03-euractiv-ag-opinion-ai-news-reuse#c4]]

## Facts
- 2026-11-03 · Advocate General Maciej Szpunar said using copyrighted works to train AI is "as a rule, freely permitted" under the text-and-data-mining exception, in a case brought by a Hungarian press publisher against Google over Gemini. He advised declaring the questions about that case inadmissible. [[2026-11-03-euractiv-ag-opinion-ai-news-reuse#c1]] [[2026-11-03-euractiv-ag-opinion-ai-news-reuse#c3]]
- 2026-11-03 · In the same opinion, Szpunar said chatbots can infringe the press publisher's right when they access articles unlawfully or systematically reuse their style of expression, and that the operator is liable if this is inherent to its business model or it failed to prevent it.
