## Current state
The Commission is due to present the Digital Fairness Act on 11 November 2026, according to Rapporteur. [[2026-11-03-euractiv-dfa-social-media-switching#c5]] It proposed the Kids Act in September 2026 and has told member states to pause overlapping national social media laws for minors for 12 months. [[2026-11-03-politico-kids-act-national-bans-paused#c1]] [[2026-11-03-politico-kids-act-national-bans-paused#c4]] heise reports that it is working, like Germany's BSI, on criteria for when a cloud provider meets which security level. [[2026-11-03-heise-merz-google-cloud-sovereign#c7]] Its Digital Omnibus proposal is in negotiation: member states agreed a mandate on 2026-10-21 and deleted its change to the definition of personal data and its "unconditional right to object" for AI training. [[2026-11-03-council-digital-omnibus-mandate#c2]] [[2026-11-03-council-digital-omnibus-mandate#c8]]
