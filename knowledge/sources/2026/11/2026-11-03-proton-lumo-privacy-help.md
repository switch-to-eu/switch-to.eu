**Summary:** Proton's support page on privacy in Lumo ([[services/proton-lumo]]). All claims describe Proton's own practices. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Proton says Lumo does not use chats to train AI models.
- `c2` The exception is feedback a user explicitly chooses to submit on an Apertus 1.5 response, which is anonymized and shared with Proton and the university research teams behind Apertus.
- `c3` Proton names the models as Qwen 3.5, GLM 5.3, Image-Turbo, FireRed-Image-Edit-1.1 and Apertus 1.5, and says they run exclusively on servers Proton controls.
- `c4` Under its no-logs policy, Proton says query data is erased after Lumo generates a response.
