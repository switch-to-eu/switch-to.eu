**Summary:** TechCrunch report on the launch of Staan, the search index built by the Qwant and Ecosia joint venture EUSP, used by [[services/qwant]] and [[services/ecosia]].

**Claims**
- `c1` On 2025-08-06, Qwant and Ecosia said both had started serving search queries through Staan.
- `c2` EUSP aimed to serve around 50% of French queries and 33% of German queries by the end of 2025.
- `c3` Qwant said it was using the index to power some features, such as AI summaries.
- `c4` EUSP was pitching Staan to companies, including AI chatbot makers, as a cheaper alternative to Google's and Bing's search APIs.
