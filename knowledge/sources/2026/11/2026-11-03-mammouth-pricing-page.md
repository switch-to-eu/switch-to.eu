**Summary:** Pricing for Mammouth, a subscription that gives access to many third-party AI models in one interface. Prices were shown in US dollars, excluding VAT; no euro price appeared. The homepage says "from $10/month", the pricing page starts at $12. No date on the page, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Starter costs $12 per month excluding VAT, Standard $24 and Expert $72.
- `c2` Every plan gives access to models from Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT), Meta (Llama), xAI (Grok), Mistral, Google (Gemini), DeepSeek, Qwen and Kimi.
- `c3` Web and deep research run through Perplexity; image and video generation through Nano Banana, GPT, Stable Diffusion, Recraft, FLUX, Sora, Veo and Kling.
- `c4` Plans include projects, voice chat and dictation, and a few dollars of API credits plus Mammouth Code.
- `c5` Team plans cost the same per user as individual plans and add an admin console.
- `c6` No free plan is listed on the pricing page.
