**Summary:** Proton's help article listing what each personal plan includes, with prices in euros. It covers storage for [[services/proton-drive]] and [[services/proton-mail]]. Drive Plus is not on this page. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Proton Drive Free gives up to 5 GB: the account starts at 2 GB and rises to 5 GB after the Drive-specific "get started" actions.
- `c2` Proton Mail Free has its own storage of up to 1 GB (500 MB, raised to 1 GB through the Mail get-started actions).
- `c3` Proton Unlimited costs €12.99/month, or €119.88 for one year (€9.99/month), with 500 GB of storage shared between Mail and Drive.
- `c4` Proton Duo costs €19.99/month, or €179.88 for one year (€14.99/month), with 2 TB shared by up to 2 users.
- `c5` Proton Family costs €29.99/month, or €287.88 for one year (€23.99/month), with 3 TB shared by up to 6 users.
- `c6` Paid bundles include Proton Mail, Calendar, Drive, Pass, VPN and Wallet.
- `c7` Plans can be changed at any time from the account settings at proton.me.
