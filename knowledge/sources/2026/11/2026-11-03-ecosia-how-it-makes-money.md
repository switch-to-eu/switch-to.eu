**Summary:** Ecosia's short explanation of its business model for [[services/ecosia]]. Last updated 2025-07-14 according to the page.

**Claims**
- `c1` Ecosia describes itself as a not-for-profit social business, not a charity, that puts 100% of its profits into climate action, mainly tree planting.
- `c2` Ecosia earns money when users click ads in its results; ads come through Google AdSense and the Microsoft Advertising network.
