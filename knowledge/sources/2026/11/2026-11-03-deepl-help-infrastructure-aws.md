**Summary:** DeepL's help article on its move to a hybrid infrastructure with Amazon Web Services as sub-processor in 2026. The page says it was translated from English with DeepL Pro. No date on the page, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` DeepL runs a hybrid infrastructure: its own servers in European data centres plus AWS cloud infrastructure, with AWS acting as a sub-processor.
- `c2` In Europe, services run on a combination of DeepL infrastructure and AWS; in other regions on AWS only. By default, customer content may be processed in different regions.
- `c3` DeepL says text entered or uploaded is processed to provide the service and not stored, unless the user saves glossaries or translations.
- `c4` DeepL says paid-subscription data is not used for training models outside the user's account, is encrypted in transit and at rest, and that DeepL, not AWS, controls the encryption keys.
- `c5` For transfers DeepL relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.
- `c6` A data residency add-on is available to sales-assisted Team, Business and API for Business customers and is standard in DeepL for Enterprise.
- `c7` Customers who object to the terms that add AWS can keep using DeepL until the end of their billing period, or the end of 2026 at the latest, with their data kept on DeepL infrastructure.
