**Summary:** OpenAI's help article on custom instructions in [[services/chatgpt]], including where to find them and whether they are in the data export. No date was readable on the page, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Custom instructions are available on all plans on web, desktop, iOS and Android.
- `c2` On web and desktop they are under Settings, Personalization, in the Custom Instructions field; on mobile under Settings, Customize ChatGPT.
- `c3` Free and Go users can save up to 1,500 characters; Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education users up to 5,000.
- `c4` OpenAI says custom instructions are included in the ChatGPT data export.
- `c5` When an OpenAI account is deleted, custom instructions tied to it are deleted within 30 days.
- `c6` OpenAI says information from custom instructions is also used to improve model performance, subject to the user's opt-out.
