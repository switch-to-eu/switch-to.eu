**Summary:** Vivaldi's desktop help page on the built-in tracker and ad blocker in [[services/vivaldi]]. The Android and iOS help pages describe the same three levels. The page does not say which level a new installation starts with. First published 2020-03-24, no last-updated date shown; read on 2026-11-03.

**Claims**
- `c1` The blocking level for all sites is set in Settings > Privacy and Security > Tracker and Ad Blocking, with three choices: No Blocking, Block Trackers, or Block Trackers and Ads.
- `c2` The tracker blocker uses lists of known tracker URLs and stops requests to them before they are sent.
- `c3` Vivaldi recommends blocking trackers on all sites and ads only on sites where they are intrusive, so that sites relying on ad revenue are supported.
- `c4` The level can be changed per site with the shield (Content Blocker) button in the address field, or through an exceptions list.
- `c5` Vivaldi says its built-in blocker makes ad-blocking extensions unnecessary, while extensions remain an option.
- `c6` Turning on Block Trackers and Ads plus the two "Remove cookie warnings" lists under Manage Sources hides cookie banners on most sites.
