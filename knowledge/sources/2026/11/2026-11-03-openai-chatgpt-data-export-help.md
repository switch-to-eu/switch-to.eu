**Summary:** OpenAI's help article on exporting data from [[services/chatgpt]]. It does not name the files inside the ZIP. No date was readable on the page, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Self-service export from settings is available for ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus, Pro and eligible Edu workspaces, but not for Business, Enterprise or ChatGPT for Healthcare workspaces.
- `c2` On the web, the path is profile menu, Settings, Data controls, then Export under "Export data", then Confirm export.
- `c3` On iOS and Android, the path is Settings, Data controls, Export data; the app may send the user to the web to finish.
- `c4` Users can also request a copy via the Privacy Portal (privacy.openai.com): Make a Privacy Request, I have a consumer ChatGPT account, Download my data.
- `c5` ChatGPT sends an email or SMS when the export is ready; it can take up to 7 days to arrive.
- `c6` The download link expires 24 hours after it is received, and the user must be signed in to the same account to download.
- `c7` The downloaded ZIP file includes chat history and other relevant account data.
- `c8` An export cannot restore chats that were already deleted.
- `c9` If the export has not arrived, OpenAI says to check the inbox, spam and promotions folders, wait up to 7 days before contacting support, and request a new export if the link expired.
