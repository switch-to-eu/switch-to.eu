**Summary:** Second read of Infomaniak's guide to [[services/euria]], for the parts [[2026-11-03-infomaniak-euria-getting-started]] left out. It names model families for Infomaniak's AI, so "the pages do not say which models" is only true of which model answers a given chat. No date on the page, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` The guide says Infomaniak's AI is currently based by default on models that include Mistral, Whisper, DeepSeek, Llama and Granite.
- `c2` The guide does not say which model answers a given Euria chat.
- `c3` The message quota is shared across projects and calculated over a rolling 24-hour window. The guide gives no number of messages.
- `c4` The guide says no data is processed or shared with any third party.
- `c5` The guide lists a document upload limit of 20 MB per file on my kSuite and my kSuite+, and 10 MB per image. Infomaniak's price page says up to 100 MB [[2026-11-03-infomaniak-my-ksuite-prices#c3]].
- `c6` Euria is not available in about 60 listed countries, none of them in the EU.
