**Summary:** A full reading of the OpenAI FAQ already noted in [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-retirement-faq]], about retiring custom GPTs in [[services/chatgpt]]. It repeats the main facts and adds what happens to connected apps, the selected model, custom actions and sharing. No date was readable on the page, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Custom GPTs are scheduled to retire on December 11, 2026. FedRAMP workspaces and affected Enterprise workspaces with a qualifying, approved deferral retire on February 11, 2027.
- `c2` At the applicable retirement date, custom GPTs and their GPT pages become inaccessible.
- `c3` Creators start migration from My GPTs by selecting "Migrate to plugin" when migration is available for their account. Migration access may become available at different times.
- `c4` During migration the GPT's instructions become a skill, its knowledge files are copied into the plugin's reference files, and connected apps are added to the plugin as apps.
- `c5` Migration uses the GPT's latest published version. Drafts and unpublished edits do not transfer, and the selected model does not carry over.
- `c6` Custom actions, which connect a GPT to another service, do not transfer. OpenAI says rebuilding the connection may require a custom MCP server and technical setup.
- `c7` An individual migration starts with a private plugin, existing GPT users do not gain access automatically, and making a personal plugin public requires a separate plugin submission process.
- `c8` After migration the original GPT remains usable until retirement but becomes read-only. OpenAI says a migrated plugin may respond differently and advises comparing familiar prompts.
- `c9` Existing conversations with custom GPTs remain accessible after retirement, and users do not need to take action to preserve them.
- `c10` People who only use someone else's GPT do not need to migrate it and may not migrate it. Access to the original does not automatically give access to a replacement. OpenAI advises checking the notice on the GPT and the creator's guidance.
