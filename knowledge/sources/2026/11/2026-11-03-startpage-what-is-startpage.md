**Summary:** Startpage's own description of [[services/startpage]], including its ownership and where its results come from. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used. Claims about its practices are Startpage's statements.

**Claims**
- `c1` Startpage says it was founded in 2006 in the Netherlands.
- `c2` Startpage says it is owned by a Dutch company headquartered in The Hague and is part of System1, a publicly traded company based in the United States.
- `c3` Startpage says it partners with Google, Bing and other providers for its search results and applies its anonymising process to each search.
- `c4` Startpage says it makes money from contextual ads and does not save, sell or share personal data.
- `c5` Startpage offers Anonymous View, a proxy for visiting result pages without the site seeing the user.
