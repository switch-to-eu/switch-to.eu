**Summary:** Second read of Ecosia's help article on AI, for the parts of [[services/ecosia-ai-chat]] that [[2026-11-03-ecosia-ai-help]] left out: the FAQ, the feature limits and the training answer. No date on the page, so the fetch date is used. Claims about data handling are Ecosia's statements.

**Claims**
- `c1` The FAQ says Ecosia does not sell or share chat data with third parties, and does not use chats for training models "since we do not train models ourselves".
- `c2` The page does not say whether Mistral or another model provider trains on the chats it receives.
- `c3` Ecosia says it uses several models side by side, including Mistral and open-weight models, per type of query. It names no chat model.
- `c4` AI Chat cannot generate videos, share conversations with other people or run a deep research mode.
- `c5` Uploads are limited to 5 files per conversation and 5 MB per file, and files are deleted within 7 days.
- `c6` Modes are Web Source (live web), Guided Learning and Think Longer.
- `c7` Users who are not signed in can still use AI Chat, with a more limited experience. Chat history and image generation need an Ecosia account.
- `c8` Auto-routing sends some searches to AI Chat. It can be disabled the first time it triggers.
- `c9` The page says Ecosia directs 100% of its profits toward climate action.
- `c10` An FAQ answer on self-hosted open source models says questions and answers stay within Ecosia's infrastructure, while the privacy section says chat requests are forwarded to Mistral. The page does not reconcile the two.

**Quotes**
> "We also do not use them for training models, since we do not train models ourselves." (Ecosia Help Center)
