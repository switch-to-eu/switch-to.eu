**Summary:** Google's help article on Google Takeout, the export tool used to get files out of [[services/google-drive]] and other Google products. It covers delivery methods, archive type and size, timing, expiry and download limits. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Downloading data with Takeout does not delete it from Google's servers.
- `c2` On takeout.google.com, products the user has data in are preselected; the user unticks what to leave out and clicks "Next step".
- `c3` Delivery options are a download link by email, or adding the archive to Google Drive (where it counts toward storage), Dropbox, Microsoft OneDrive or Box.
- `c4` File type is .zip or .tgz; archives larger than the chosen maximum size are split into several files, and choosing the 50 GB limit reduces splitting.
- `c5` Creating the archive can take from a few minutes to a few days; Google says most people get the link the same day.
- `c6` An archive expires after about 7 days and can be downloaded at most 5 times; after that a new archive is needed.
- `c7` The archive may miss changes made between the request and its creation, including changes to sharing type or permissions of Drive files.
- `c8` Google recommends downloading and opening Takeout exports on a desktop computer; each archive contains an archive_browser.html file describing its formats.
- `c9` Google Workspace administrators can block users of work or school accounts from downloading data.
