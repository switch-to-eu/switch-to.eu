**Summary:** A thread on Vivaldi's own forum where users asked why their default search engine in [[services/vivaldi]] switched to Startpage, with answers from a member of the Vivaldi Team (staff badge). The staff replies do not name the regions where Startpage became the default. Staff statements are rated medium; user posts in the thread are opinion. Thread started 2024-12-13; read on 2026-11-03.

**Claims**
- `c1` A Vivaldi Team member said on 13 December 2024 that Vivaldi had changed the default search engine in selected regions from Bing to Startpage, because it no longer had a deal with Bing.
- `c2` The same staff member said search engine partnerships are integral to Vivaldi's revenue and fund the browser's development.
- `c3` The staff member said the change applied only to users who had never changed their search settings, and that a manually chosen search engine is not affected.
- `c4` One user reported that after switching back to Bing, Vivaldi occasionally showed prompts asking them to reconsider Startpage.
