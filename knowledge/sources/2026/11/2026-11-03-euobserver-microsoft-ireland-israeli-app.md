**Summary:** A report by the campaign group Ekō, published on 3 November 2026, says Microsoft hosts key components of Al Munasseq, an app Israel's COGAT unit uses to manage Palestinians' movement permits, in data centres in Ireland. An Irish MP called for an investigation. Only the opening of the article was readable (paywall).

**Claims**
- `c1` Al Munasseq is an app launched in 2019 by Israel's Coordination of Government Activities in the Occupied Palestinian Territory (COGAT) unit to manage the movement of Palestinians for work, healthcare, prayer and everyday tasks.
- `c2` A report published on 2026-11-03 by the campaign group Ekō says Microsoft hosts key components of Al Munasseq in data centres in Ireland.
- `c3` Ekō's senior campaign director Rewan Al-Haddad said that if a Western tech company can host such a system in Ireland, inside the EU, without consequences, other companies will learn they can do the same.
- `c4` Sinead Gibney, a member of the Irish parliament's AI committee, called for an investigation in light of the report.
