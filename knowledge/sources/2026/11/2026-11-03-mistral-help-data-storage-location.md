**Summary:** Mistral's help article on where it hosts user data for its products, including Vibe ([[services/mistral-le-chat]]). The page carries the date August 12, 2026.

**Claims**
- `c1` Mistral says data is hosted in the European Union by default.
- `c2` Data is hosted in the United States only when a customer explicitly uses Mistral's US API endpoint.
- `c3` Mistral says contracts with service providers that process personal data outside the EU include GDPR Article 46 safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
- `c4` Mistral says subprocessors outside the EU undergo a security review, sometimes with extra measures such as zero data retention or encryption.
