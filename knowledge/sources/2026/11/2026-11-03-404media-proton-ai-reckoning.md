**Summary:** Investigation by Joseph Cox into Proton's push for AI-written code under CEO Andy Yen, based on employee interviews, Slack messages, meeting recordings, a CTO manifesto and an internal document. The article is paywalled after the opening, so the claims below come only from the readable part. That part describes internal concerns about building security products with AI-written code and does not report a breach of user data. This is the original reporting behind [[2026-11-03-heise-proton-slack-leak]].

**Claims**
- `c1` 404 Media spoke to eight current and one former Proton employees and reviewed Slack chats, meeting recordings, an AI manifesto written by the company's CTO and an internal document.
- `c2` According to messages 404 Media saw, at the end of August 2026 Yen replied to an employee who urged the company to take a few days to pentest newly written code before release: "That's bullshit and loser thinking." In a follow-up message he wrote "only losers think like that".
- `c3` 404 Media reports that Yen told engineers to stop handcrafting code and use AI more.
- `c4` 404 Media reports that Proton installed software on workers' computers that tracks how much AI code the company generates.
- `c5` 404 Media reports that Yen said this is the last year Proton will hire engineers at all.
- `c6` Proton said it would re-interview staff about their use of AI.
- `c7` An internal document says that "due to insufficient velocity" Proton is not on track to reach its annual recurring revenue target.
- `c8` A Proton spokesperson did not dispute any of 404 Media's reporting. According to 404 Media, the spokesperson said Proton has at times been too blunt or too slow in explaining its direction to staff.

**Quotes**
> "That's bullshit and loser thinking." (Andy Yen in internal Slack, August 2026, as reported by 404 Media)
