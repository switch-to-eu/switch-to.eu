**Summary:** Google's help article on the shared storage quota behind Gmail, [[services/google-drive]] and Google Photos. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Each Google Account includes up to 15 GB of storage shared across Gmail, Google Drive and Google Photos.
- `c2` Files in shared folders count only against the original owner's quota, never the recipient's.
- `c3` Docs, Sheets, Slides and other collaborative files created or edited after 1 June 2021 count against the quota; files last edited before that date do not.
- `c4` Storage use per service can be checked at drive.google.com/settings/storage.
- `c5` After deleting many files, it normally takes 48 to 72 hours for the storage figure to update.
