**Summary:** Mammouth's explainer on what happens to prompts sent through it to third-party model providers. Claims about provider behaviour are Mammouth's reading of the providers' terms.

**Claims**
- `c1` Mammouth says it is a company based in Europe, does not train any internal models and never uses conversations to train anything.
- `c2` Prompts are stored so users can revisit their history and can be deleted at any time.
- `c3` Prompts, relevant history, document extracts and custom instructions are sent to the model provider that answers; Mammouth says identity, user ID and chat IDs are not sent.
- `c4` Mammouth says it enforces two guarantees across providers: no training on prompts from Mammouth, and no retention beyond 30 days, generally for abuse monitoring.
- `c5` Named providers and routes: OpenAI API, Mistral paid API, Anthropic commercial terms, Gemini through Google Cloud, Fireworks for open models (DeepSeek, Llama, GLM, Kimi), and OpenRouter as a routing layer.
