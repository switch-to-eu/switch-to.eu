**Summary:** OpenAI's help article on custom GPTs in [[services/chatgpt]]. It announces that custom GPTs will be retired and replaced by plugins, and that personal accounts can no longer create new GPTs. No date was readable on the page, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` OpenAI plans to retire custom GPTs and move creators to plugins; standard retirement is scheduled for December 11, 2026.
- `c2` New GPT creation and publishing are not available on personal ChatGPT accounts, including Free, Go, Plus and Pro.
- `c3` A GPT combines instructions, conversation starters, knowledge files, capabilities, and apps or actions.
- `c4` GPTs do not use saved memory, custom instructions or previous conversations.
- `c5` For consumer plans, GPT conversations may be used for training unless the user opts out.
