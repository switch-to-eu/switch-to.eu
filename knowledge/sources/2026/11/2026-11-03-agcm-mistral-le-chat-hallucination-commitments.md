**Summary:** Decision of Italy's competition and consumer authority, adopted on February 17, 2026, in consumer-protection case PS12968 against Mistral AI SAS over how Le Chat ([[services/mistral-le-chat]]) informed users about AI hallucinations. The authority made Mistral's commitments binding and closed the case without finding an infringement. Translated from Italian.

**Claims**
- `c1` The AGCM opened proceedings PS12968 against Mistral AI SAS on June 3, 2025, over insufficient information to users about the risk of hallucinations.
- `c2` Mistral offered commitments on July 29, 2025, supplemented on October 8, 2025.
- `c3` The commitments include a permanent Italian-language disclaimer in the Le Chat web and app chat windows warning that answers may contain errors, with a link to the terms of service.
- `c4` Mistral also committed to translating its terms of service and the whole mistral.ai site, including the help centre, into Italian.
- `c5` On February 17, 2026, the AGCM made the commitments binding and closed the proceedings without establishing an infringement.
- `c6` Mistral had to report on implementation within 120 days of notification; the case can be reopened if the commitments are not implemented.
- `c7` The decision states that Le Chat has been available via web and app in Italy since February 6, 2025, and actively promoted there since May 29, 2025 through a partnership with Iliad Italia.
