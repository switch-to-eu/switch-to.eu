**Summary:** Privacy policy for Black Forest Labs' websites, Playground, APIs and models. It says it was updated but no date appeared in the text Jina returned, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` BFL may use prompts, uploaded images, tasks and generated outputs to train and improve its AI models, after data minimisation and de-identification.
- `c2` Users who object can email privacy@blackforestlabs.ai with the subject "Training Opt Out"; there is no settings toggle described.
- `c3` For users in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, BFL GmbH is the data controller.
- `c4` The policy lists two entities: Black Forest Labs Inc in San Francisco and BFL GmbH in Freiburg im Breisgau.
- `c5` Data of EEA, Swiss and UK users may be transferred to and processed in other countries, under Standard Contractual Clauses.
- `c6` The policy does not cover content processed for enterprise customers, which falls under customer agreements.
