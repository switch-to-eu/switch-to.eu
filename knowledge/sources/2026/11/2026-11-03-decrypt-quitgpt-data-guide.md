**Summary:** A Decrypt step-by-step guide to exporting data from and deleting a [[services/chatgpt]] account, written during the March 2026 "QuitGPT" wave. It names the files in the ChatGPT export, which OpenAI's help page does not. Some of its other claims could not be matched to OpenAI's current help pages.

**Claims**
- `c1` Decrypt reports that the ChatGPT export ZIP contains a conversations.json file with message metadata and a browser-viewable chat.html file.
- `c2` Decrypt reports that the QuitGPT movement passed 2.5 million pledges after OpenAI's agreement with the US Department of War, announced on February 27, 2026.
- `c3` Decrypt advises feeding an export to a new assistant in structured chunks rather than as a raw JSON dump.
- `c4` Decrypt advises copying the saved-memory list by hand (screenshot or text) before deleting it.
