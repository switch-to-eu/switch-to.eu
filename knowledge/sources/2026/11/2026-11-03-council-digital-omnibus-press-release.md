**Claims**
- `c1` The Council agreed its position on 21 October 2026 and says the proposal simplifies rules "while maintaining strong levels of protection for personal data".
- `c2` The Council says the mandate "maintains the current definition of personal data and introduces a new provision on pseudonymisation and the identification of a natural person".
- `c3` The Council says AI development is supported "with clarifications on the lawful ground for processing personal data and need to maintain appropriate safeguards", and that the mandate adds safeguards to the derogation for sensitive data in biometric verification and AI.
- `c4` The Council says it keeps the ePrivacy rules on cookies and adds purposes where consent is not required, such as providing a service, audience measurement or device security, "thus reducing the number of cookie banners".
- `c5` The Council presidency starts negotiations with the European Parliament once Parliament adopts its own negotiating position.
- `c6` The Commission's proposal is dated 19 November 2025.
- `c7` The "One Europe, One Market" roadmap, signed by the presidents of the Council, Parliament and Commission on 24 April 2026, includes the objective of concluding work on all simplification packages by the end of 2027.
