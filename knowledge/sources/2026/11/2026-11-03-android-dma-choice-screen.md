**Summary:** Google's page on the search and browser choice screens it shows in the EEA to comply with the Digital Markets Act, on Android and in [[services/chrome]]. Updated 2024-03-29 according to the page. Relevant to [[services/google-search]].

**Claims**
- `c1` Google says the European Commission designated it a gatekeeper under the DMA on 2023-09-06, and from March 2024 it shows choice screens to comply.
- `c2` On Android phones and tablets, users must choose a search provider during initial device setup.
- `c3` Choosing a provider on the Android search choice screen sets the home screen search box, sets Chrome's default search engine (if Chrome is installed), and installs the provider's app if needed.
- `c4` Android also shows a browser choice screen during setup; the chosen browser is installed if needed and becomes the default for links.
- `c5` The screens reached eligible Pixel users in the EEA in early March 2024; from 2024-03-06, Android OEMs must include the choice screen software in new devices released in the EEA.
- `c6` Google says it encourages, but does not say it requires, OEMs to bring the choice screen to existing devices.
- `c7` Google also shows a search choice screen in Chrome on other operating systems.
