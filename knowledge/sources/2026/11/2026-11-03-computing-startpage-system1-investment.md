**Summary:** Trade press report from 2020 on System1's investment in [[services/startpage]], with statements from Startpage and its CEO Robert Beens. Trade press, so reliability is medium; statements are the company's.

**Claims**
- `c1` Startpage is owned by Surfboard Holding BV, a Dutch company, and System1 invested in it through a subsidiary called Privacy One Group.
- `c2` Startpage's CEO declined to give the percentage of shares held by System1.
- `c3` Startpage said decisions on privacy practices are held by its co-founders, so System1 cannot change them.
- `c4` Startpage's CEO said US personnel cannot access machines that link to the anonymising servers, only machines where personal data has already been removed.
- `c5` The System1 deal led privacytools.io to delist Startpage; it was later relisted with a warning.
