**Summary:** Qwant's steps for making [[services/qwant]] the default in [[services/chrome]] without its extension. The page said "3 months ago Updated" when fetched; no exact date is shown, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Qwant says to visit qwant.com and perform at least one search first, which registers Qwant in Chrome's list of available search engines.
- `c2` Then open the Chrome menu > Settings > Search engine and select Qwant under "Search engine used in the address bar".
- `c3` Qwant says its Chrome extension makes the same settings in one click.
