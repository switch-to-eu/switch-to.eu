**Summary:** At the German government's Digital Summit in Berlin, Chancellor Friedrich Merz called digital sovereignty an existential question in the age of AI, then defended plans by the German intelligence services to build their new offline cloud with Google as project partner. He said Google invests in Germany and Europe and that the setup is "a European infrastructure". heise notes that no US hyperscaler cloud, including air-gapped ones, runs for long without vendor updates.

**Claims**
- `c1` In his speech at the Digital Summit in Berlin on 2026-11-02, Chancellor Friedrich Merz described digital sovereignty as Germany making and enforcing its own decisions in line with its values and security interests, and said this does not mean striving for autarky.
- `c2` Merz said digital sovereignty becomes an "existential question" in the context of AI.
- `c3` heise reports that the German intelligence services apparently want to build their new offline cloud with Google as a project partner.
- `c4` Asked about those plans, Merz said Google also invests in Germany and Europe and that "we can also enable European sovereignty with an American provider here".
- `c5` Merz said the setup "is not a fully integrated infrastructure, it is a European infrastructure", and that he has high trust that the data held there is safe, even when an American provider builds a large cloud infrastructure.
- `c6` The Chancellery, which Merz heads, also coordinates the German intelligence services.
- `c7` heise reports that Germany's federal cyber security agency BSI and the European Commission are working on detailed criteria for when a cloud provider meets which security level for which use.
- `c8` heise writes that no cloud offering from a US hyperscaler runs for long without updates from the vendor, including the "air-gapped" cloud the German services want, so a replacement would have to be found within months if updates stopped.

**Quotes**
> "The central question of the economic order is no longer: make or buy. It is: who sets the rules." (Friedrich Merz, translated from German)
> "I have high trust in our services that the data we have there is also safe, protected, even if an American provider builds a large cloud infrastructure, but it is safe for us." (Friedrich Merz, translated from German)
