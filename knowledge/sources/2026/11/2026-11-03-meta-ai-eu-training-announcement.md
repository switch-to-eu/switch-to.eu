**Summary:** Meta's newsroom post announcing AI training on EU users' public content and Meta AI interactions. Dated 2025-04-14 and updated 2026-03-27.

**Claims**
- `c1` Meta says it will train its AI models in the EU on people's interactions with AI at Meta and on public content shared by adults on its products.
- `c2` People's questions and queries to Meta AI will also be used to train and improve its models.
- `c3` People in the EU can object to their public data being used for training through a form linked from notifications.
- `c4` Meta says it does not train on private messages with friends and family unless someone in the chat shares those messages with its AI features; an update on 2026-03-27 says messages shared with AI features may be used for training.
- `c5` Public data from EU accounts of people under 18 is not used for training.
- `c6` Meta AI launched in the EU in March 2025 inside Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Messenger.
