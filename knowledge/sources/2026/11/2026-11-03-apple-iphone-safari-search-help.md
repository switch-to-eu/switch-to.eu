**Summary:** Apple's iPhone User Guide page (iOS 27 edition at fetch time) on searching from Safari. Its "Choose a search engine" section gives the path Settings > Apps > Safari, which differs from the path on Apple's dedicated page ([[2026-11-03-apple-iphone-default-search-engine-help]]). The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Under "Choose a search engine", the steps are: open Settings, tap Apps, tap Safari, tap Search Engine, then choose a search engine.
- `c2` Search engine suggestions can be turned off under Settings > Apps > Safari > Search Engine Suggestions.
- `c3` The page does not list which search engines are available in Safari.
