**Summary:** DeepL's blog post announcing that it adds AWS as a sub-processor and stops processing data only in Europe.

**Claims**
- `c1` DeepL says it will no longer process data exclusively within Europe.
- `c2` DeepL is adding AWS as a sub-processor, giving global scale and performance as the reason.
- `c3` DeepL says AWS will not control or access customer data in any usable form and that customer data from paid services is not used to train its AI models.
- `c4` DeepL offers Bring Your Own Key encryption, with which customers can revoke access to their data.
- `c5` DeepL says it will work with customers who have data residency requirements so that their data never leaves Europe.
