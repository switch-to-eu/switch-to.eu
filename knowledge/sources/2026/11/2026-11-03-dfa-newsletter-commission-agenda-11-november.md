**Summary:** Specialist newsletter post (James Tamim) that publishes the Commission's agenda planning list of 29 September 2026, which puts the Digital Fairness Act on the College meeting of 11 November 2026, and stresses that the date is indicative. MLex reported the same agenda on the same day. Use it to word the 11 November date as planned, not fixed.

**Claims**
- `c1` The Commission's agenda planning list of 29 September 2026 ("Liste des points prévus pour figurer à l'ordre du jour des prochaines réunions de la Commission") puts a consumer package, with the Digital Fairness Act and a consumer enforcement initiative, on the College meeting of 11 November 2026.
- `c2` The author writes that the date is indicative, not final: the meeting date is yet to be confirmed and the President can change the schedule.
