**Summary:** Pricing for Lara Translate, an AI translation service. The page offers a euro and dollar switch, but the text Jina returned showed only US dollar prices. Its privacy policy link points to lara.translated.com. No date on the page, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` The Free plan allows 60,000 characters per month, no credit card, plus a free API allowance of 10,000 characters per month.
- `c2` Pro costs $9.99 per month billed annually for 500,000 characters per month and adds mobile apps.
- `c3` Team costs from $29.99 per month billed annually for 1.5 million shared characters with unlimited users.
- `c4` Lara says it translates in 200+ languages and 70+ file formats with document layout preserved.
- `c5` Data sovereignty and residency is listed only on the Enterprise plan.
