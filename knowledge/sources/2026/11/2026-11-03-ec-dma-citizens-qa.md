**Summary:** The European Commission's Q&A for citizens on the Digital Markets Act. Used for its plain description of choice screens. The page shows no single date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` The Commission tells consumers that choice screens make it easier to choose the browser and search engine that is best for them.
- `c2` The Commission says Mozilla reported Firefox usage on iOS rose by about 100% in France and Germany after the DMA browser choice screen was introduced.
- `c3` In April 2026, the Commission sent Google preliminary findings proposing that third-party search engines get access to Google Search ranking, query, click and view data on fair, reasonable and non-discriminatory terms.
