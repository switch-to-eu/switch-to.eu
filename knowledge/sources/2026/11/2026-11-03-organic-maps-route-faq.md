**Summary:** The official FAQ entry on routing in [[services/organic-maps]]. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used. It may predate the June 2026 public transport release.

**Claims**
- `c1` A destination can be picked from search, from bookmarks or by tapping the map, followed by the "Route to" button.
- `c2` Route types are Car, Pedestrian, Subway, Bike and Ruler.
- `c3` Routes can be previewed from another starting point, but navigation only runs from the user's current location.
- `c4` A route can have up to 100 intermediate stops.
- `c5` Car routes can avoid tolls, unpaved roads, motorways and ferries via Settings > Routing options.
