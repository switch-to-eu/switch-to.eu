**Summary:** Proton's short FAQ on access, price and storage for [[services/proton-drive]], with prices in euros. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Drive Plus costs "as little as €4.99 per month" and includes 200 GB of storage.
- `c2` Proton Unlimited is listed at €12.99 per month with 500 GB of storage.
- `c3` On all paid plans, Proton Drive storage is shared with the Proton Mail account.
- `c4` Proton Free has a maximum of 6 GB split between the two services: 1 GB for Mail and 5 GB for Drive.
- `c5` Storage cannot be bought for Proton Drive alone; more storage requires upgrading the plan.
- `c6` Proton Drive is reachable at drive.proton.me, through the app switcher in Proton Mail, and through the Android and iOS apps.
