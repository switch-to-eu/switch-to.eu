**Summary:** Mistral's help article on whether Vibe conversations in [[services/mistral-le-chat]] are used for training. The page says "Updated over 2 weeks ago" without an exact date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Vibe input and output are used by default to train Mistral's models unless the user opts out.
- `c2` Enterprise customers are opted out of training by default.
- `c3` On every plan, giving thumbs-up or thumbs-down feedback with a comment authorises Mistral to use that rating, input and output to improve its models.
