**Summary:** The official FAQ entry on sharing or exporting bookmarks and tracks from [[services/organic-maps]]. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` A single bookmark or track is shared with the Share button on its place page.
- `c2` A whole list is exported from the Bookmarks and Tracks screen via the three-dot icon next to the list name, as KMZ, GPX or GeoJSON.
