**Summary:** Google's list of what a Takeout export of [[services/google-drive]] contains, per product. It does not list default export formats for Docs, Sheets or Slides. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Only files that have not been deleted can be downloaded; work or school accounts may not be able to download some data.
- `c2` Exporting files larger than Drive's maximum size limits may make the export fail.
- `c3` For Drive, the export includes files and folders, the current version of each file, versions marked "Keep forever", trashed content, file comments, and metadata such as stars, descriptions and folder colours.
- `c4` For Docs, the export includes text, open and resolved comments and suggestions, named revisions, images (possibly at lower quality), drawings, charts and tables.
- `c5` For Sheets, the export includes cell content and formulas, comments, notes, charts and named revisions; for Slides, slide content, comments, embedded video and audio, and named revisions.
- `c6` The Drive section also lists shared drive names and shared drive resource IDs with subfolder names.
