**Summary:** Google's rules for which search engines appear on its EEA search choice screens on Android and in [[services/chrome]], and in what order. No date shown, so the fetch date is used. Relevant to [[services/google-search]], [[services/qwant]], [[services/ecosia]] and [[services/startpage]].

**Claims**
- `c1` A provider must run a general web search service, offer a free app in Google Play to EEA users with at least 5,000 installs, and apply and be approved.
- `c2` Providers owned by or affiliated with the same company can appear only once per country, and providers under the control of non-EEA state actors are excluded.
- `c3` Options are chosen per country from Google Play install data and shown in random order; users must scroll through all options before choosing.
- `c4` On new EEA devices distributed from 2024-08-07, the Android screen shows the top 8 ranked search engines in random order.
- `c5` On iOS and desktop, Chrome does not show the search choice screen if the user has already selected a non-Google search engine.
- `c6` Google says appearing on the choice screen is free for providers.
