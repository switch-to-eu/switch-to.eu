**Summary:** French tech news report of a GDPR complaint filed with the CNIL against Mistral AI over the training opt-out in the free version of Le Chat ([[services/mistral-le-chat]]). Translated from French. A search on November 3, 2026 found no public CNIL decision on the complaint.

**Claims**
- `c1` In February 2025, lawyer Jérémy Roche filed a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL) against Mistral AI.
- `c2` The complaint objected that free Le Chat users had no opt-out from having their inputs and outputs used for model training, while Pro subscribers could switch it off.
- `c3` The lawyer argued that tying the opt-out to a paid plan breaches GDPR Article 12, which says exercising data rights must be free of charge.
