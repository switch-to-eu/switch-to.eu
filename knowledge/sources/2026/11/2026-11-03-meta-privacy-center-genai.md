**Summary:** Meta's Privacy Center page on AI training, read logged out in the browser from Europe. It shows no date, so the fetch date is used. Everything below is Meta's own description of its practice. The forms themselves need a login and were not seen. The objection form is reached from the page's "Your right to object" section. Compare [[2026-11-03-meta-ai-eu-training-announcement#c3]], the 2025 announcement.

**Claims**
- `c1` Meta says that in the European Region and the UK it relies on legitimate interests to collect and process personal information in its sources of training data to develop and improve AI at Meta.
- `c2` Meta says people have the right to object to their interactions with AI at Meta features and the public information they shared on Meta Products being used to develop and improve AI at Meta, through a request from their Facebook, Instagram or Meta account.
- `c3` Meta says an objection applies to the account you are logged into and to accounts in the same Accounts Center. A separate request covers messages with AIs on WhatsApp.
- `c4` Meta says anyone, whether or not they use Meta Products, can submit requests about their personal information from third parties being used for AI at Meta.
- `c5` Meta says it sends an email confirming it won't use public information or AI interactions for future development and improvement of its generative AI models.
- `c6` Meta says that if an objection arrives after a training run has started, it may still use the information for that run, but not for future training.
- `c7` Meta says you may need to be logged in to see the forms for your region. On 2026-11-03 the page linked to https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/6359191084165019 (Facebook) and https://help.instagram.com/contact/233964459562201 (Instagram).
