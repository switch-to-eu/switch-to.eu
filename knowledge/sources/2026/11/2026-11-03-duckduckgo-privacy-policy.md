**Summary:** The privacy policy of [[services/duckduckgo]]. The page says "Last updated 06-03-26", read as 2026-06-03 (US date format). Claims about its practices are DuckDuckGo's statements.

**Claims**
- `c1` DuckDuckGo says it does not save or share users' search, chat or browsing history.
- `c2` DuckDuckGo says it uses information such as IP address only temporarily to deliver content and for security, and saves search queries only without identifiers.
- `c3` DuckDuckGo says its search ads are based on the results page, not the person, and ad clicks are managed by Microsoft's ad network.
- `c4` DuckDuckGo says devices typically connect to regional servers, for example European servers in Europe.
- `c5` DuckDuckGo says it has never sold personal information.
