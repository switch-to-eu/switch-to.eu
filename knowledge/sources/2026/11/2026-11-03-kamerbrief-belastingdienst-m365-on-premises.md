**Summary:** Government letter "Toekomstbestendigheid Belastingdienst" from State Secretary for Finance Eelco Eerenberg, dated 5 October 2026. Besides the tax office's IT planning, it reports that the Tax Administration, Customs and the Benefits Agency drop the planned move of their office workplace to Microsoft 365 and choose on-premises servers plus European open source. Read from the letter text (download at tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D48732). Translated from Dutch.

**Claims**
- `c1` The Tax Administration (Belastingdienst), Customs (Douane) and the Benefits Agency (Toeslagen) re-examined the choice made in 2025 to replace their workplace with Microsoft 365 and now choose an on-premises solution combined with open source.
- `c2` In the on-premises scenario the software runs on servers of the Tax Administration or servers it controls. The letter says this became possible partly because the Tax Administration increased its data-centre capacity.
- `c3` Mail and calendar move to on-premises servers first, in 2027. European open-source solutions for personal storage and collaboration follow later in 2027 and in 2028.
- `c4` The approach aims to use existing licences as much as possible, to keep additional investment low.
- `c5` The letter links the choice to the advice of the Adviescollege ICT-toetsing not to rely on one solution from one supplier but to look at alternatives per function.
- `c6` Some planned records-management features were part of the Microsoft 365 package and are not available on-premises. The Tax Administration is working out what is needed, and the state secretary will report back in the next progress letter at the latest.
- `c7` The letter names geopolitical developments and the resulting attention to digital autonomy as an outside factor in IT planning. For the VAT modernisation the Tax Administration takes over the hosting from the supplier, which moves the VAT refund go-live to April 2027 and the domestic VAT rollout from 1 July 2027 to the third quarter of 2027.
- `c8` Parliament receives a roadmap of the Tax Administration's IT priorities in the first quarter of 2027.

**Quotes**
