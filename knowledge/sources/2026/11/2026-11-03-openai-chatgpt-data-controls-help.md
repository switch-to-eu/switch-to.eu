**Summary:** OpenAI's help article on the model-training setting and other data controls in [[services/chatgpt]]. The page said "Updated: 4 days ago" on November 3, 2026, so the published date is set to October 30, 2026.

**Claims**
- `c1` On personal ChatGPT Free, Plus and Pro accounts, data sharing for model training is on by default.
- `c2` Users turn it off under Settings, Data controls, "Improve the model for everyone"; the setting applies across web and mobile and does not delete chats.
- `c3` Voice mode and Codex have their own separate training controls.
- `c4` During Trusted Access sessions (such as Daybreak), OpenAI may use content for training even when "Improve the model for everyone" is off.
- `c5` Temporary chats do not appear in history, do not create memories, are not used for training and may be kept up to 30 days for safety.
- `c6` If a user gives thumbs-up or thumbs-down feedback, the whole conversation may be used for training, even after opting out.
- `c7` By default, OpenAI does not train on content from Business, Enterprise, Edu or ChatGPT for Healthcare workspaces.
