**Summary:** Google's help article on importing files into Google My Maps, a Google mapping tool separate from [[services/google-maps]]. It lists accepted formats, size limits and the columns My Maps needs to place points. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` My Maps imports CSV, TSV, KML, KMZ, GPX, XLSX, Google Sheets and photos from Google Drive or Google Photos.
- `c2` Unzipped KML and KMZ files can be up to 5 MB, and other files up to 40 MB.
- `c3` A spreadsheet needs a header row and a column with latitude-longitude, addresses, place names or WKT geometry.
- `c4` Files with more than 2,000 rows should not be imported.
- `c5` To import on a computer: sign in to My Maps, open or create a map, click Add layer, then Import under the new layer, and upload the file.
