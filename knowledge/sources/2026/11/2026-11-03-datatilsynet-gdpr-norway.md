**Summary:** The Norwegian data protection authority's English page on the GDPR. It confirms the GDPR applies in Norway through the EEA Agreement, which covers Norwegian companies such as the maker of [[services/vivaldi]]. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Norway is not an EU member but is a member of the European Economic Area.
- `c2` The GDPR was incorporated into the EEA Agreement and became applicable in Norway on 20 July 2018.
- `c3` Datatilsynet says Norway is bound by the GDPR in the same manner as EU member states.
