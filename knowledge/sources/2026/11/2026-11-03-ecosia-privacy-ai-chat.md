**Summary:** The AI Chat sections of Ecosia's privacy policy, read for [[services/ecosia-ai-chat]]. The earlier note [[2026-11-03-ecosia-privacy-policy]] covers search only. The policy's last-updated date is used. Claims about data handling are Ecosia's statements. The policy says nothing on whether Mistral or OpenAI train on these chats.

**Claims**
- `c1` Ecosia says AI Chat queries, with metadata, are sent to its model providers, and that the provider it primarily uses is Mistral AI.
- `c2` Ecosia says Mistral won't store input and output, because Ecosia uses Mistral's Zero Data Retention policy.
- `c3` Ecosia says it may use OpenAI as a backup provider when Mistral is unavailable or a fallback is necessary, with transfers to the US under standard contractual clauses.
- `c4` Ecosia says OpenAI stores input and output for 30 days for quality assurance.
- `c5` Ecosia uses Langfuse to review a limited sample of conversations. The policy says sampled data is hosted in Ireland (AWS eu-west-1) and kept for up to 30 days.
- `c6` Ecosia says it regularly creates fully anonymised data sets from parts of the sampled conversations to improve response quality.
- `c7` Users opt out of service-improvement use by email to Ecosia support. The same section says Ecosia may request consent for this collection and names legitimate interest as the legal basis.
- `c8` Chat history uses Cloudflare as a subprocessor, and the policy says data may be transferred to the US even if stored in the EU.
- `c9` Uploaded files are kept for 7 days.
