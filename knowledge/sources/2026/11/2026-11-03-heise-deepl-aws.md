**Summary:** heise reports DeepL's switch to processing customer data on Amazon Web Services from 2026-05-20. English translation of a German original.

**Claims**
- `c1` From 2026-05-20, DeepL no longer processes customer data only on its servers in Germany and Iceland and adds AWS cloud services.
- `c2` The new terms count as accepted unless customers actively object; objecting customers can use their subscription until the end of the billing period, at the latest 2026-12-31.
- `c3` heise reports that DeepL had applied the new infrastructure terms to new contracts and renewals since the start of 2026.
- `c4` By default DeepL processes content in various AWS regions, including the EU, the US and Japan; only customers with special sales contracts can fix a region.
- `c5` heise notes that the US CLOUD Act applies to data processed by Amazon, a US company.
