**Summary:** Vivaldi's help page on getting data into [[services/vivaldi]] on iPhone and iPad. It covers Sync, a Safari import, and the lack of a direct import from other iOS browsers such as [[services/chrome]]. Published 2026-03-26; read on 2026-11-03.

**Claims**
- `c1` To bring data from Vivaldi on desktop or another device to iOS, Vivaldi recommends Sync (Settings > Sync, log in, enter the encryption password).
- `c2` Vivaldi on iOS can import a Safari export: in iOS Settings > Apps > Safari > History and Website Data > Export, save to Files, then in Vivaldi go to Settings > Safari import > I've exported my data > Select your file > Import.
- `c3` Vivaldi says direct import from other browsers on iOS is not possible.
- `c4` As a workaround, Vivaldi suggests syncing the other browser to its desktop version, importing into Vivaldi on desktop, and syncing Vivaldi to iOS.
- `c5` After a Safari import, Vivaldi offers to delete the exported file.
