**Summary:** The pricing page for [[services/proton-drive]], read in its yearly view. The reader rendered prices in US dollars only; a `?currency=EUR` request still showed USD. Prices below are the yearly-billing figures as displayed on 2026-11-03. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Proton Free is advertised with 5 GB of storage, an online document and spreadsheet editor, and no credit card required.
- `c2` Drive Plus: 200 GB, $3.99/month billed $47.88 every 12 months (shown against a $4.99 monthly price), with 10 years to recover previous file versions.
- `c3` Proton Unlimited: 500 GB plus Proton VPN, Mail, Calendar and Pass, $9.99/month billed $119.88 every 12 months (shown against $12.99).
- `c4` Proton Duo: 2 TB for up to 2 users, $14.99/month billed $179.88 every 12 months (shown against $19.99).
- `c5` Proton Family: 3 TB for up to 6 users, $23.99/month on yearly billing (shown against $29.99).
- `c6` Drive Professional (business) is listed with 1 TB per user at $7.99/month on yearly billing (shown against $9.99).
- `c7` Paid plans carry a 30-day money-back guarantee; Proton's FAQ says a refund within 30 days covers the unused portion, prorated.
- `c8` The FAQ says paid individual plans go up to 3 TB.
