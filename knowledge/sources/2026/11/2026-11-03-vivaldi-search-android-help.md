**Summary:** Vivaldi's help page on search in [[services/vivaldi]] for Android. First published 2019-09-09; the page may have been updated since.

**Claims**
- `c1` The default is set under Vivaldi menu > Settings > Search Engine > Standard Tab, then tap the engine.
- `c2` Private tabs have their own default under Vivaldi menu > Settings > Search Engine > Private Tab.
- `c3` The built-in list includes Ecosia (nickname e) and StartPage (nickname s).
- `c4` Custom engines are added under Vivaldi menu > Settings > Search Engine > Add Custom Search Engine, with a name, a nickname and the search URL.
