**Summary:** Infomaniak's help guide for Euria, its AI assistant, which is part of kSuite and also a standalone app. It sets out usage limits per plan. No date on the page, so the fetch date is used. Claims about data handling are Infomaniak's statements. The older URL for "understand the use of AI at Infomaniak" now redirects here.

**Claims**
- `c1` Infomaniak says its AI is hosted exclusively on its own servers in Switzerland.
- `c2` Infomaniak says results generated are never used to train the models or for advertising.
- `c3` Euria is built into Infomaniak's Mail, kChat, kDrive and Site Creator as well as available as a standalone app.
- `c4` Message limits are counted over a rolling 24-hour window shared across all projects; a single conversation cannot exceed 254,000 tokens.
- `c5` Transcription is unlimited.
- `c6` Projects per account: 2 projects with 5 conversations each on kSuite Free and my kSuite; unlimited on my kSuite+ and kSuite Business and Enterprise.
- `c7` Uploads are capped at 20 MB per document on my kSuite and my kSuite+, 100 MB on kSuite plans, and 20 files per conversation.
