**Summary:** Vivaldi's release post for desktop version 7.1 of [[services/vivaldi]]. Used here only for the section on new default search engines and the revenue partners behind them. Published 2025-01-23; read on 2026-11-03.

**Claims**
- `c1` With version 7.1 in January 2025, Vivaldi changed some of its default search engines, which it says helps keep the browser free and independent.
- `c2` Vivaldi names Startpage, Ecosia, DuckDuckGo and Qwant as the partner search engines that generate revenue for it.
- `c3` Vivaldi says it made changes so that ad attribution works, because this is needed for its partners to pay it.
- `c4` Vivaldi states it does not track or profile users or sell their data.
