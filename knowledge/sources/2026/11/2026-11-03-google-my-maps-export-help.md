**Summary:** Google's help article on sharing and exporting maps made in Google My Maps, a Google tool separate from [[services/google-maps]]. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` A whole My Maps map is downloaded on a computer via the left panel Menu > Export to KML/KMZ.
- `c2` Individual layers can be exported to CSV via the layer Menu > Export data > CSV.
- `c3` Maps can be shared by link or made public from the Share option in the left panel.
