**Summary:** The official FAQ entry on failed map downloads in [[services/organic-maps]]. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Map downloads can fail because of temporary network errors or firewall settings on a network or router, and trying later or on another Wi-Fi network may help.
- `c2` Downloads need enough free storage on the device.
- `c3` If very old maps cannot be updated, deleting and downloading them again may help.
