**Summary:** Apple's support article on the Default Apps settings in iOS and iPadOS, including where the EU browser choice screen and the Japan-only search engine choice screen apply. Published 2025-12-18 according to the page.

**Claims**
- `c1` Default apps are changed under Settings > Apps > Default Apps; "Browser App" sets which app opens web links instead of Safari.
- `c2` In EU countries and Japan, users might be prompted to choose a default browser from a web browser choice screen.
- `c3` Apple says that in Japan users might be prompted to choose a default search engine in Safari from a search engine choice screen, and can change it later under Settings > Apps > Safari.
- `c4` The article does not describe a search engine choice screen for EU users.
