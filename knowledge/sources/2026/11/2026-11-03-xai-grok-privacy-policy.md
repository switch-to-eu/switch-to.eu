**Summary:** The privacy policy for Grok's websites and apps, last updated 2026-10-27. The operator is now named SpaceX Super Intelligence LLC ("SpaceXSI"); x.ai hosts the policy.

**Claims**
- `c1` SpaceX Super Intelligence LLC calls itself a US-based company and says it developed Grok, a conversational AI.

- `c3` It uses publicly available internet data to train its models.
- `c4` With Private Chat on, conversations do not appear in history and are deleted within 30 days unless needed for legal, compliance or safety reasons.
