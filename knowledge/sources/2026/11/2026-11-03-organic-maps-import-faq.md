**Summary:** The official FAQ entry on importing bookmarks and tracks into [[services/organic-maps]], for single files and for whole folders. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Organic Maps imports bookmarks and tracks in KML, KMZ, KMB, GPX and GeoJSON (JSON) format, from Organic Maps or from third-party apps.
- `c2` A single file is imported by tapping it (from email, a messenger or cloud storage) and choosing "Open with Organic Maps" on Android or "Import with Organic Maps" from the share icon on iOS.
- `c3` After a successful import the app shows "Bookmarks loaded successfully!" and the items appear on the map and in the Bookmarks and Tracks screen.
- `c4` For a batch import, the user opens Bookmarks and Tracks (the stars-with-dots button), taps "Import Bookmarks and Tracks" and selects a folder.
- `c5` On Android, the batch import scans the selected folder and its subfolders for supported files.
