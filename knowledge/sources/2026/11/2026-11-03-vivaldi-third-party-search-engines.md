**Summary:** Vivaldi's page linking the privacy policies of the search engines bundled with [[services/vivaldi]]. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Vivaldi says the search engines included by default depend on the browser's language settings.
- `c2` The page lists Bing, DuckDuckGo, Ecosia, Qwant, Startpage, Yahoo! and Yandex as third-party search engines included in Vivaldi.
- `c3` The full list of third parties used to build Vivaldi is shown at vivaldi://credits in the browser.
