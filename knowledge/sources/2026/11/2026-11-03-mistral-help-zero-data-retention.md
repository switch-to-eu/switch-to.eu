**Summary:** Mistral's help article on zero data retention. It confirms that ZDR is not available in Vibe ([[services/mistral-le-chat]]) and explains when conversations are removed. The page carries the date August 12, 2026.

**Claims**
- `c1` Zero Data Retention is available only with pay-as-you-go and only for stateless API calls.
- `c2` ZDR is not available for Vibe Work or Chat on any plan, because Mistral stores data to show conversation history and application state.
- `c3` Mistral says conversations are removed when the user deletes them or deletes the account, typically after a short grace period for accidental-loss, monitoring and legal-retention purposes.
- `c4` Vibe users can manage data-training preferences in privacy settings instead.
