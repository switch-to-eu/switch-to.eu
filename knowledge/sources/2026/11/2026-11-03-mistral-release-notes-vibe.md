**Summary:** Mistral's product release notes. The newest entry is dated October 30, 2026. The Vibe entries of September 22, 2026 describe the merge of Chat and Work in [[services/mistral-le-chat]] (now Vibe) and the replacement of Memories and Agents.

**Claims**
- `c1` In a release note dated September 22, 2026, Mistral says Chat and Work are merging into one Vibe experience, with a phased rollout from September 14, 2026 for Free, Pro and Teams accounts.
- `c2` Enterprise migration starts October 1, 2026, with administrators choosing a date within an approximately six-month window.
- `c3` Skills replace Chat Agents, and Deep Research is available as a Skill.
- `c4` Chat Memories migrate into a knowledge topic in the Knowledge Base.
- `c5` Temporary chat starts a conversation without adding it to history or the Knowledge Base.
- `c6` Code Interpreter in Work is available on paid plans.
- `c7` Spreadsheets in Vibe Work (Excel and CSV analysis, .xlsx download) are available on paid plans.
- `c8` The Knowledge Base (Public Preview) includes a guided onboarding interview to bootstrap it when no external sources are connected.
