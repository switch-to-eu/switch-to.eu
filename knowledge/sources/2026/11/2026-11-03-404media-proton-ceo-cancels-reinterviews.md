**Summary:** Follow-up by Joseph Cox to [[2026-11-03-404media-proton-ai-reckoning]]. A day after the investigation, Andy Yen cancelled the planned company-wide AI "re-interviews". Paywalled after the first paragraphs; claims come from the readable part only.

**Claims**
- `c1` On Friday 30 October 2026, Proton CEO Andy Yen cancelled a near company-wide round of "re-interviews" that would have required employees to explain how they use AI, according to six current employees and audio of an all-hands meeting.
- `c2` The cancellation came a day after 404 Media published its investigation into Yen's push for AI despite security objections.
- `c3` Users criticised Proton's leadership on social media and called for Yen to resign.
- `c4` One employee told 404 Media they did not expect Yen to change anything.
