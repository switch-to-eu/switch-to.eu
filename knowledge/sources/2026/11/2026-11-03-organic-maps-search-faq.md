**Summary:** The official FAQ entry on failed searches in [[services/organic-maps]]. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` To search in an area, the user needs the map for that area downloaded and either zoomed in on it, be located near it, or name the country or town in the search.
- `c2` A place may not exist yet in OpenStreetMap, and users can add it there.
- `c3` If a place shows on the map but search does not find it, the project asks users to email its coordinates and the search query.
