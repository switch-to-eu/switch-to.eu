**Summary:** Qwant's legal notice, required under the French Consumer Code, on how [[services/qwant]] indexes and ranks results. The URL redirects to Qwant's help center. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Qwant indexes web pages with its own crawlers, called "Qwantify".
- `c2` Qwant says some of its results come from third-party indexing services, and names Microsoft Bing's programming interfaces among them.
- `c3` Qwant says no commercial agreement influences the ranking of web results; ads are labelled "Advertisement" or "Partnership".
- `c4` Shopping offers on Qwant come only from Microsoft partner merchants, and Qwant is paid by Microsoft when a user clicks one.
- `c5` Qwant says the order of results is not influenced by the user's search or browsing history.
