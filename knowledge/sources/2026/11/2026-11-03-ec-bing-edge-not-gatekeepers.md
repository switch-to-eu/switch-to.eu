**Summary:** Commission news item from 2024-02-13 on its decision that [[services/bing-search]] and [[services/microsoft-edge]] are not gatekeeper services under the DMA, so DMA choice-screen duties do not attach to them.

**Claims**
- `c1` On 2024-02-12, the Commission decided that Microsoft's search engine Bing, web browser Edge and Microsoft Advertising, and Apple's iMessage, should not be designated as gatekeeper services.
- `c2` The Commission said it will keep monitoring these services for substantial changes.
- `c3` The decisions do not affect the designation of Apple and Microsoft as gatekeepers from 2023-09-05.
