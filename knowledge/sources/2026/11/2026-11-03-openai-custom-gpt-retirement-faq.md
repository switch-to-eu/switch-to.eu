**Summary:** OpenAI's FAQ on retiring custom GPTs in [[services/chatgpt]] and migrating them to plugins. No date was readable on the page, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Custom GPTs are scheduled to retire on December 11, 2026; FedRAMP workspaces and some Enterprise workspaces with an approved deferral retire on February 11, 2027.
- `c2` At the retirement date, custom GPTs and their GPT pages become inaccessible.
- `c3` Creators migrate a GPT from My GPTs with "Migrate to plugin"; the GPT's instructions become a skill and its knowledge files are copied into the plugin's reference files.
- `c4` Migration uses the GPT's latest published version; drafts and unpublished edits do not transfer, and custom actions do not transfer.
- `c5` After migration the original GPT becomes read-only.
- `c6` Existing conversations with custom GPTs remain accessible after retirement.
- `c7` Users of someone else's GPT cannot migrate it and do not automatically get access to its replacement.
- `c8` New GPT creation ended on October 26, 2026 for affected Enterprise workspaces, except FedRAMP workspaces.
