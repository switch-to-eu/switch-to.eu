**Summary:** A personal blog post about moving to [[services/vivaldi]] on macOS and Android, updated until 2026-02-03. One user's experience; useful for pitfalls to test, not as proof. Published 2026-01-30; read on 2026-11-03.

**Claims**
- `c1` The author says the import from other browsers worked during onboarding, but found no way to import Chrome profiles other than the main one.
- `c2` The author reports a bug where dragging a playing tab to a second screen made it disappear while it kept playing, and worked around it by turning off Enable Drag and Drop Tiling under Settings > Tabs.
- `c3` The author turned off Accent from Page (Themes > Editor) to stop the tab colour changing per site, and turned off Use Tab Zoom to keep zoom levels per site.
- `c4` The author says syncing to Vivaldi on Android started straight away and Send to Your Devices moved tabs between laptop and phone.
- `c5` The author reports installing a Manifest V2 Chrome extension from the Chrome Web Store in January 2026.
