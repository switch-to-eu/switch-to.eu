**Summary:** The homepage of CoMaps, a community fork of [[services/organic-maps]]. Statements about privacy and governance are the project's own. CoMaps is not a listed service on switch-to.eu. Date taken from the page metadata.

**Claims**
- `c1` CoMaps describes itself as a fork of Organic Maps and Maps.Me, driven by an open-source community, with code on Codeberg.
- `c2` CoMaps offers offline search and turn-by-turn routing on foot, by bike and by car, with voice announcements.
- `c3` CoMaps says it does not identify or track people and collects no information, and links to an Exodus Privacy report.
- `c4` CoMaps has a public transport layer for subway and light rail journeys in a growing list of cities.
- `c5` CoMaps says it comes preinstalled as the default map app on CalyxOS and iodéOS.
