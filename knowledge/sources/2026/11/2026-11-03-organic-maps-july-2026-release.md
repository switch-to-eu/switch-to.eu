**Summary:** Release notes for the July 2026 version of [[services/organic-maps]], mostly fixes after the June public transport release.

**Claims**
- `c1` The release notes say live schedules for public transport routes are still in development.
- `c2` Route warnings for tolls, ferries, unpaved roads and steps are shown for all alternate routes.
- `c3` iOS gained a setting to turn off and hide search history.
- `c4` On Android, opening hours now show split shifts and the whole week.
- `c5` Notes reported to OpenStreetMap from the app are placed on the exact spot the user selected.
