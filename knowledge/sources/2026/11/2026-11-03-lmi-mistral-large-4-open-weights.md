**Summary:** Mistral AI released the weights of Mistral Large 4, a multimodal model with 1,000 billion parameters, on Hugging Face under its own MIT-derived open-weight licence. Mistral says it is the best open model developed in the West and highlights coding and cybersecurity uses. Publishing the weights lets organisations run the model in their own environment.

**Claims**
- `c1` Mistral AI made the weights of Mistral Large 4, nicknamed "Le Chonk", available on Hugging Face. The multimodal model has 1,000 billion parameters, 52 billion of them active.
- `c2` Large 4 is also available through Mistral's API and can be tried in Vibe Code. It was presented on 2026-10-06 and got three more weeks of reinforcement training before the weights were released, adding three reasoning levels (low, medium, high).
- `c3` Mistral presents Large 4 as the best open model developed in the West. According to Mistral, it uses on average 47% fewer tokens per task than GLM-5.3 in agentic coding.
- `c4` Publishing the weights lets companies and public administrations run the model in their own environment rather than relying only on a remote service.
- `c5` Large 4 was trained on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's European data centres.
- `c6` The weights are published under the Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight License, a licence derived from the MIT licence that sets conditions on redistribution and commercial use.
- `c7` CEO Arthur Mensch said Mistral has raised about $6 billion in three years.
