**Summary:** DeepL's plan comparison for its translator (Free, Individual, Team, Business, Enterprise). The per-plan prices did not render in the text Jina returned; only the Write Pro add-on price did. No date on the page, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` The free version allows 50,000 characters of text translation per user per month; Individual allows 300,000, Team 1,000,000, Business and Enterprise unlimited under a fair usage policy.
- `c2` File translation: 1 file per month on the free version, 3 on Individual, 20 on Team, 100 on Business.
- `c3` The DeepL Write Pro add-on costs €6.99 per user per month billed annually, VAT included.
- `c4` DeepL says prices include VAT and that annual billing saves 16%.
- `c5` DeepL says Pro subscribers' original content and results are stored only as technically needed and then deleted, and are never used to train DeepL's AI models.
- `c6` DeepL says it holds ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certified processes and infrastructure.
- `c7` Individual and Team plans have a 30-day free trial.
