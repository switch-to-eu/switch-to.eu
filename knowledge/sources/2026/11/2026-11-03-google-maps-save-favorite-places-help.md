**Summary:** Google's help article on saving places to lists in [[services/google-maps]] (Computer variant). It states the size limit for saved lists and that saving requires a Google Account. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Saved lists are limited to 3,000 entries, and lists with more entries may not display properly.
- `c2` Saving and finding favourite places requires signing in to a Google Account.
- `c3` Saved places are visible only to the user unless they share a list, and Google may share combined information about users' saved places anonymously.
- `c4` On a computer, saved places are found under Saved at the top left of Google Maps.
- `c5` Places can also be saved from Google Maps embeds on other websites, and these show with the website name in Google Maps.
