**Summary:** Proton's help article on moving a Google Photos library to [[services/proton-drive]] through a separate Google Takeout export. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Google Photos is exported separately in Google Takeout by ticking Google Photos after "Deselect all"; Proton names .zip and a 2 GB file size as example settings.
- `c2` Google emails a download link that Proton says stays usable for one week.
- `c3` Takeout can scatter photos from the same period across parts; Proton suggests the largest archive size, or sorting photos into folders by year before import, because the Windows app builds albums from folder structure.
- `c4` On the web, unzipped folders are dropped into the Photos tab, and Proton Drive ignores files that are not images or videos.
- `c5` The Windows app has Photos import → Import from Google Photos, which uploads, sorts photos into albums and skips duplicates.
