**Summary:** Proton's getting-started article for [[services/proton-drive]] on web and mobile, including a storage table per plan and upload options per platform. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` A storage table lists Proton Free at 5 GB, Mail Plus at 15 GB, Drive Plus at 200 GB and Proton Unlimited at 500 GB.
- `c2` In the web app, files and folders can be dragged into the window, or picked with "New upload", "Upload file" or "Upload folder".
- `c3` Several items downloaded from the web app at once are saved as a ZIP file.
- `c4` In the mobile apps, uploads start from Files → + → "Upload a file" (Android) or "Import file" (iOS).
- `c5` "Upload a folder" is offered on Android only.
