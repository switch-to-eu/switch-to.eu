**Summary:** Ecosia's help article on AI Overviews and AI Chat in [[services/ecosia]]. No date on the page, so the fetch date is used. Claims about data handling are Ecosia's statements.

**Claims**
- `c1` Ecosia Search has two generative AI features, AI Overviews and AI Chat. AI Chat is a beta feature available only to some users.
- `c2` AI Overviews are powered by Staan through EUSP and use a Mistral Small 3.2 model. They are on by default and can be switched off in settings.
- `c3` Ecosia forwards AI Chat requests to its external chat provider, Mistral, and says it uses a mix of open-weight models and Mistral.
- `c4` Conversations are kept in the browser's local storage; saving chat history on Ecosia's servers is opt-in and encrypted.
- `c5` AI Chat is available in 35 listed countries, including most EU member states, the UK, Switzerland, Norway and the US.
- `c6` Image generation in AI Chat, for signed-in users only, uses Black Forest Labs. Ecosia says BFL does not keep the data beyond processing or use it for training, and generated images are deleted after 7 days.
- `c7` Ecosia says it prefers models hosted by European companies within the EU, outside Ireland, to keep users outside the scope of the US CLOUD Act.
- `c8` An AI-free search setting that removes AI Overviews, AI Chat and auto-routing is available on desktop; mobile support is planned.
