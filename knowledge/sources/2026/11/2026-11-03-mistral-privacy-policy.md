**Summary:** Mistral AI's consumer privacy policy, covering Vibe ([[services/mistral-le-chat]]) and Mistral AI Studio. The policy says "Effective: September 3, 2026".

**Claims**
- `c1` Mistral AI, a French company registered in Paris under number 952 418 325, is the data controller for consumer use of its products.
- `c2` Mistral keeps Vibe input and output until the user deletes the conversation or the account.
- `c3` Account data such as the email address used to verify or change a password is kept for 1 year after account deletion.
- `c4` Mistral says it prioritises providers in the EU but may use non-EU providers in exceptional cases, with Standard Contractual Clauses attached to those contracts.
- `c5` Mistral lists its main providers on its Trust Center.
- `c6` Users have account controls to delete their account, obtain a copy of their data, and object to the use of their input and output for model training.
- `c7` Users can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, including the French CNIL.
- `c8` Users can access, add, delete or edit their Memories or Knowledge Base at any time in settings.
