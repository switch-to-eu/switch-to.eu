**Summary:** Qwant's page on its iOS app, a browser with [[services/qwant]] as its search engine. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` The Qwant app is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad and needs iOS 15 or later.
- `c2` Qwant describes the app as a full browser with tabs, favourites and private mode.
- `c3` The app has a "Zap" feature that closes all tabs and deletes cookies set during browsing.
- `c4` Once installed, the app can be set as the iPhone's default browser so links from other apps open in it.
