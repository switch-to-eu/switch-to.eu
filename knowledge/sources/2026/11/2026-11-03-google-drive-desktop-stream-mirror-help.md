**Summary:** Google's advanced guide to Drive for desktop for [[services/google-drive]], read for streaming versus mirroring and offline access. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Streaming is the default mode: files stay in the cloud and use local space only when opened or made available offline.
- `c2` Mirroring stores a full copy of My Drive on the computer, and all files are available offline.
- `c3` Shared drives always use streaming.
- `c4` To use Google Docs, Sheets and Slides offline, they must be made available offline in Drive on the web.
- `c5` Making a large number of files available offline at once can cause errors such as "too many open files"; Google suggests smaller batches.
- `c6` Folders synced from the computer, listed under "Folders from your computer", use mirroring.
