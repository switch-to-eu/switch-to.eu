**Summary:** Trade blog on Microsoft message center post MC1269223, which turns on "flex routing" for Microsoft 365 Copilot in EU and EFTA tenants. Applies to work tenants, not the consumer Copilot app.

**Claims**
- `c1` From 2026-04-17, flex routing lets Microsoft process Microsoft 365 Copilot inference for EU and EFTA tenants outside the EU Data Boundary during peak demand.
- `c2` The blog says requests may then be routed to data centres in the United States, Canada or Australia.
- `c3` Microsoft says data at rest stays inside the EU Data Boundary, except limited pseudonymised data kept outside it for security and operational purposes.
- `c4` Flex routing is on by default for tenants created after 2026-03-25 and for all others from 2026-04-17; admins can switch it off in the Microsoft 365 admin center.
- `c5` The setting does not appear for tenants with Multi-Geo or with a sign-up location outside the EU and EFTA.
