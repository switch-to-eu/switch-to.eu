**Summary:** The [[services/organic-maps]] team's review of 2025: releases, download counts, GitHub activity and a user poll on missing features. Self-reported figures.

**Claims**
- `c1` The team says lifetime downloads at the end of 2025 were almost 2M on the App Store and almost 3M on Google Play, plus an estimated 1M+ from other Android sources, around 6M in total.
- `c2` Organic Maps shipped 13 app releases in 2025.
- `c3` In a poll of nearly 1,000 users on the most requested missing feature, Public Transport ranked first, Traffic Info second and Better Search third.
- `c4` In 2025 the app added hiking and cycling routes from OpenStreetMap and showed bus numbers at bus stops as a first step toward public transport navigation.
- `c5` The team reports 1.5K commits from 100 contributors in 2025.
- `c6` Users downloaded 10 petabytes of map data from the project's servers in 2025.
