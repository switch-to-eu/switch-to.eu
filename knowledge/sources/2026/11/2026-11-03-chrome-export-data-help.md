**Summary:** Google's help article on downloading [[services/chrome]] data stored in a Google Account through Google's data download tool. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Chrome data stored in a Google Account while signed in can be downloaded as an archive with Google's data download tool.
- `c2` The Chrome export can include autofill, bookmarks, browser history, dictionary, extensions, search engines and settings.
- `c3` Saved passwords are not in this export. Google points to Password Manager settings at passwords.google.com/options to export them.
- `c4` Payment information is stored in Google Wallet and exported separately.
