**Summary:** Mistral's pricing page for Vibe, the renamed [[services/mistral-le-chat]], fetched from a US location. The page uses the name Vibe throughout and only mentions Le Chat in the student-offer terms. The price widget stores the same number for euro and dollar (for example `priceEur: 14.99`). The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Plans: Free, Pro, Team and Enterprise.
- `c2` Pro costs €14.99 or $14.99 per month, shown excluding taxes, with a toggle to show prices including taxes per country.
- `c3` Team costs €24.99 or $24.99 per user per month, excluding taxes.
- `c4` Verified students get Pro for €5.99 or $5.99 per month, for at most 12 months, if they study at an accredited higher-education institution and have not used Vibe (or Le Chat) before.
- `c5` The Free plan gives limited messages and web searches, basic image generation, access on web and mobile, and 100+ connectors.
- `c6` Pro and Team allow up to 6x the Free messages, up to 5x the web searches, up to 40x the image generations and up to 1,000 project folders. Pro gets up to 15 GB of libraries and document uploads, Team up to 30 GB per user.
- `c7` The interfaces listed are Vibe on web, Vibe CLI, Vibe for IDE and Vibe on mobile (iOS and Android).
- `c8` In the "Model training" row, the Pro, Team and Enterprise columns say "Opt-out". The Free column does not say "Opt-out" and shows the same icon the table uses for included features (for example the Free "Help center" cell).
- `c9` Chat and email support is listed for paid plans, and "Data export" is listed under team and enterprise features.
- `c10` The FAQ says Team gets 200 Flash answers per day and Pro 150.
