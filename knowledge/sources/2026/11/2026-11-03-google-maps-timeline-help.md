**Summary:** Google's help article on Timeline in [[services/google-maps]], read in its Android, iPhone & iPad and Computer variants (same answer ID, different `co=` parameter). Timeline now lives on each device, with an optional encrypted backup, and is exported from the phone, not from Takeout or a computer. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Timeline saves visits and routes on each signed-in device, and an optional backup stores an encrypted copy on Google's servers.
- `c2` The Computer variant says Timeline is not available for Maps on a computer, because its data comes directly from the device.
- `c3` On Android, Timeline data is exported from the phone's Settings app: Location > Location services > Timeline > Export Timeline data, then Continue, a storage location and Save.
- `c4` On iPhone and iPad, Timeline data is exported in the Google Maps app via the profile picture > Settings > Location & Privacy > Export Timeline data, then Save to Files.
- `c5` The iOS export is saved as a file named location-history.json.
- `c6` Timeline is off by default and only turned on when the user opts in; on iOS, turning it on means consenting to regular saving of each device's precise location, also when Google apps are closed.
- `c7` Deleted Timeline data cannot be recovered, and Timeline data may still exist on other devices and in backups.
- `c8` Timeline data older than 3, 18 or 36 months can be deleted automatically.
- `c9` With Search Services History or Web & App Activity on, Google may keep other location data in the account even after Timeline is turned off or deleted.
- `c10` On Android, Timeline is turned off in the Google Maps app via the profile picture > Your Timeline > More > Location & privacy settings > Timeline is on, then Turn off or Turn off and delete activity.
