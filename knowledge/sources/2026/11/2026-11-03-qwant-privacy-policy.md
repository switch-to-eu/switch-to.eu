**Summary:** Qwant's privacy policy for [[services/qwant]] search. The URL redirects to Qwant's help center. No date was found on the page, so the fetch date is used. Claims about Qwant's own practices are Qwant's statements.

**Claims**
- `c1` Qwant says search without consent uses no targeted advertising, keeps no search history and sets no advertising cookies on the user's device.
- `c2` Qwant says it partners with Microsoft for search results "when we do not ourselves have the answers" and for contextual ads based on keywords and region.
- `c3` For that partnership, Qwant may send Microsoft the search keywords, browser information, the first three bytes of the IP address, an approximate region or city, a salted hash and a random token.
- `c4` Qwant says it may also send Microsoft the full IP address for security purposes such as spam and click-fraud detection.
- `c5` Qwant says Microsoft keeps the full IP address of Bing queries for 6 months and cross-session identifiers for 18 months.
- `c6` Qwant lists "European Perspective (Staan)" as a partner alongside Microsoft for processing queries, and Mistral AI with European Perspective for AI summaries.
- `c7` With the user's consent, Qwant allows Microsoft Advertising to use device data for personalised ads.
