**Summary:** The privacy policy of [[services/ecosia]], last updated 2026-05-21 according to the page. Claims about Ecosia's own practices are Ecosia's statements.

**Claims**
- `c1` Ecosia says it does not compile user data to create a personal profile.
- `c2` Ecosia says the user's IP address and search terms are automatically shared with its search partners, Microsoft Bing or Google, when the user searches.
- `c3` Ecosia says it anonymises IP addresses after a maximum of 7 days.
- `c4` Ecosia says eligible users get Google results by default, and can choose Microsoft Bing as their search provider in settings instead.
- `c5` Ecosia says ads are not personalised by default; personalised ads from Bing or Google, and Microsoft Clarity session analytics, require consent.
- `c6` According to Ecosia's policy, Bing anonymises IP addresses after 6 months and cookie IDs after 18 months.
- `c7` Ecosia uses Cloudflare (US) for spam protection and says Cloudflare receives anonymised and obfuscated IP addresses.
