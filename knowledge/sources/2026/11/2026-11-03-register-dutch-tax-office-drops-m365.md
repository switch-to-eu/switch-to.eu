**Summary:** The Register (Richard Speed) on the Dutch letter in [[2026-11-03-kamerbrief-belastingdienst-m365-on-premises]], with background on how far the Microsoft 365 rollout had gone and why the government's ICT review board advised against it. Use the letter itself for the decision and the timeline.

**Claims**
- `c1` The Microsoft 365 rollout had reached about 5,000 of 47,500 employees across the tax, customs and benefits services.
- `c2` The Dutch Advisory Council on ICT Assessment (Adviescollege ICT-toetsing) recommended rolling back the rollout in June 2026, saying the Microsoft 365 public cloud design "falls short on information security, information processing and future-proofing".
- `c3` According to The Register, the board's criticism included confidential documents travelling via American servers, standard Microsoft encryption instead of Double Key Encryption, and end-to-end encryption that was not on by default for one-to-one Teams calls and unavailable for group calls.
- `c4` The board also pointed to vendor lock-in, reduced sovereignty and the lack of an exit strategy.
