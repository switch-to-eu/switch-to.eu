**Summary:** Proton's dated changelog of shipped [[services/proton-drive]] features. The newest entry is dated 2 October 2026, used here as the published date. No Google Drive importer and no Linux desktop app appear among the shipped items.

**Claims**
- `c1` On 9 June 2026, Proton introduced the Proton Drive CLI for Windows, macOS and Linux, to upload and download files, manage folders and sharing, and automate workflows.
- `c2` On 4 June 2026, Proton says its rebuilt Drive SDK made uploads up to 3x faster and downloads up to 2x faster on all platforms.
- `c3` On 1 July 2026, iOS gained a replace, keep-both or skip choice for uploads with a duplicate name; Proton says Android support comes later.
- `c4` On 21 August 2026, Proton Docs gained a table of contents and export of any past version as DOCX.
- `c5` On 26 January 2026, Proton wrote that the SDK work leads, among other things, "ultimately" to a Linux client.
- `c6` The newest entry, dated 2 October 2026, covers pinch-to-zoom in the iOS photo grid.
