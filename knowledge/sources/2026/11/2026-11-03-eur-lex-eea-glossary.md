**Summary:** The EU's official glossary entry for the European Economic Area. Used to state plainly that Norway, home of [[services/vivaldi]], is in the EEA but not the EU. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` The EEA groups the 27 EU member states with three EFTA countries: Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway.
- `c2` The EEA is a single market with the same rules on the free movement of goods, capital, services and persons, competition and related areas.
- `c3` The EEA Agreement entered into force on 1 January 1994.
- `c4` The EEA Agreement does not cover the customs union, common trade policy, common foreign and security policy, justice and home affairs, taxation, or economic and monetary union.
