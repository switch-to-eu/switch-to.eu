**Summary:** Ecosia's help article on where [[services/ecosia]] search results and ads come from: Microsoft Bing, Google and the EUSP index built with [[services/qwant]]. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Ecosia says its results and related ads come from Microsoft Bing, Google and EUSP (European Search Perspective).
- `c2` Which provider is used depends on the user's location, device type, and the cookie and privacy permissions granted, and it can change between searches.
- `c3` Ecosia says Bing results are available to all users, Google results only in specific regions and browsers, and EUSP results only in France.
- `c4` Users can see which provider served a result by clicking the three dots or information icon next to it.
- `c5` Users can influence the provider through the market (region) and, where available, a search provider preference in Ecosia's search settings.
- `c6` Ecosia says it does not reorder the organic results returned by its partners, and ads (Microsoft Advertising or Google AdSense for Search) do not affect organic ranking.
