**Summary:** DeepL's privacy policy, covering the free and paid versions of DeepL Translator and DeepL Write and its apps. No version date was found in the text Jina returned, so the fetch date is used. Claims about DeepL's handling of texts are DeepL's statements.

**Claims**
- `c1` For the free versions of DeepL Translator and DeepL Write, DeepL processes submitted texts and their translations or improvements for a limited period to train and improve its neural networks and algorithms, including corrections users make.
- `c2` DeepL's terms do not allow texts containing personal data of any kind in the free Translator or Write; that is only possible with a DeepL Pro subscription.
- `c3` For DeepL Translator Pro, API Pro and Write Pro, submitted texts are kept only as long as needed to produce the result, then deleted, and are not used to improve DeepL's services.
- `c4` DeepL offers desktop apps for Windows and macOS and mobile apps for iOS and Android, in free and Pro versions.
- `c5` Data about each document translation (not the content) is stored in a database and deleted automatically after 14 days.
- `c6` Translation Memory content and its embeddings are used only within the user's account and are not used to train DeepL's models.
