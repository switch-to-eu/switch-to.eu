**Summary:** Google's help article on signing in to and out of [[services/chrome]] with a Google Account, what signing in adds, and how to delete synced data from the account (computer version read). The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Signing in to Chrome with a Google Account makes bookmarks, passwords and more available on all the user's devices.
- `c2` A signed-in Chrome signs the user in automatically to Gmail, YouTube, Search and other Google services.
- `c3` If Web & App Activity is on and Chrome history is synced, Google uses it to personalise other Google products.
- `c4` Signed-in users can use AI Mode side-by-side browsing to chat about open tabs or files.
- `c5` To delete saved info from the Google Account, open chrome.google.com/data in Chrome and select Delete data.
- `c6` Signing out is done via Profile > Sign out of Chrome.
