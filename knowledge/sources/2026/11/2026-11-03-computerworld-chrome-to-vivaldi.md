**Summary:** An opinion column by JR Raphael about switching from [[services/chrome]] to [[services/vivaldi]] on Android and Windows. It focuses on getting used to the interface, not on the data move. Published 2026-03-11; read on 2026-11-03.

**Claims**
- `c1` The author says moving data from Chrome to Vivaldi was easy, and that the hard part was getting used to Vivaldi's desktop interface.
- `c2` The author says switching on Android was painless, while the desktop switch took him several more months.
- `c3` To make the desktop version feel closer to Chrome, the author hid the vertical tab bar, which he describes as on by default, set Tab Cycling to "Cycle in Tab Order" and set New Tab Position to "After Related Tabs".
- `c4` The author recommends trying one new Vivaldi feature at a time instead of all at once.
- `c5` The column cites StatCounter estimates of Chrome at about three-quarters of worldwide desktop browser use in early 2026.
