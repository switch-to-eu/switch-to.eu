**Summary:** Mistral's help article on opting out of model training for Vibe, the renamed [[services/mistral-le-chat]], on web and mobile. The page carries the date September 3, 2026.

**Claims**
- `c1` Vibe users are not opted out of training by default and can opt out at any time in their settings.
- `c2` Vibe Enterprise customers are opted out by default; Vibe Teams administrators can disable training for the whole organisation.
- `c3` On the web, users open the Admin panel (admin.mistral.ai), select Vibe under Manage, and under Privacy disable "Allow your interactions to be used to train our models".
- `c4` On iOS and Android, users open Settings, then Data & Account Controls under Account, and deselect "Enable data sharing".
- `c5` Documents uploaded in Vibe count as input data and fall under the same training setting.
- `c6` The Vibe and API (Studio) training opt-outs are separate toggles.
