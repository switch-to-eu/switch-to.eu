**Summary:** Scaleway's product page for its pay-per-token AI model APIs, aimed at developers and businesses. No date on the page, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Scaleway says it does not collect, read, reuse or analyse the content of inputs, prompts or outputs sent to the APIs.
- `c2` Prices are per million tokens in euros; for example mistral-small-3.2 costs €0.15 input and €0.35 output per million tokens.
- `c3` Hosted models include Mistral, Qwen, DeepSeek, Gemma, Llama, gpt-oss and Whisper (audio transcription at €0.003 per minute).
- `c4` The model playground links use the fr-par (Paris) region.
