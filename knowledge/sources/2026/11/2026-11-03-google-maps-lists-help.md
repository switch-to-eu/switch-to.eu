**Summary:** Google's help article on lists in [[services/google-maps]], read in its Android and Computer variants. It explains private, shared and collaborative lists and says lists are exported through Google Takeout by ticking "Saved". The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` On Android, a new list is either Private (only the owner can view and edit) or Shared (a link to view or edit).
- `c2` A shared list can have invited editors, and anyone who can view it sees the names and profile pictures of people who joined it.
- `c3` To export saved lists, the user goes to takeout.google.com, keeps "Saved" ticked in "Create a new export", picks a destination, frequency and file format, and creates the export.
- `c4` Lists made by someone else can be saved by the user and then appear under "Lists you saved".
- `c5` The Computer variant says starred places cannot be shared.
- `c6` In the Android app, lists are found under the You tab; on a computer, under Saved.
- `c7` On a computer, a list is deleted from Saved via the More button next to the list, then Delete list.
