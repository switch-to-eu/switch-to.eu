**Summary:** Qwant's announcement of European Search Perspective (EUSP), a joint venture with Ecosia to build a European search index used by [[services/qwant]] and [[services/ecosia]]. Company blog about itself, so reliability is medium.

**Claims**
- `c1` EUSP is based in Paris and owned 50/50 by Ecosia and Qwant.
- `c2` EUSP operates outside Ecosia's steward-owned model, so it can raise outside investor capital.
- `c3` The companies said both had historically relied on Microsoft's Bing, and Ecosia had switched to a mix of Google and Bing results the year before.
- `c4` EUSP planned to start serving Qwant's and Ecosia's France-based traffic by Q1 2025, and a significant portion of traffic in Germany by the end of 2025.
- `c5` Both firms said they would keep their existing back-end search partnerships alongside the new index.
- `c6` The index is meant to be available to other search engines and technology companies.
