**Summary:** Release notes for the June 2024 version of [[services/organic-maps]], which added iCloud sync of bookmarks on iOS and GPX export.

**Claims**
- `c1` iOS users can sync bookmarks and tracks through iCloud after enabling it in the app settings, and the app offers a backup first.
- `c2` Bookmarks and tracks can be exported as GPX, in addition to KML/KMZ.
- `c3` On Android, bookmarks and tracks can be exported to local device storage.
- `c4` Search finds US zip codes and UK postcodes, using TIGER and Ordnance Survey address data.
- `c5` Turn instructions can announce street names once enabled in settings.
