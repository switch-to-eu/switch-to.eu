**Summary:** DuckDuckGo's legal help page on privacy rights for [[services/duckduckgo]] users. Last updated 6-17-2026 according to the page. It shows DuckDuckGo treats the GDPR as applying to it.

**Claims**
- `c1` DuckDuckGo names GRCI Law Limited in Drogheda, Ireland, as its representative under Article 27 of the GDPR.
- `c2` DuckDuckGo names GRCI Law Limited in Ely, UK, as its representative under the UK GDPR.
- `c3` DuckDuckGo tells users they can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority and links the list of EU authorities.
