**Summary:** DeepSeek's privacy policy for its chat service, last updated 2026-02-10, with an addendum for the EEA, Switzerland and the UK.

**Claims**
- `c1` The services are provided and controlled by Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd., registered in China.
- `c2` DeepSeek says it directly collects, processes and stores personal data in the People's Republic of China.
- `c3` One purpose is to train and improve its technology, including machine learning models, by monitoring interactions and usage.
- `c4` Users have a right to opt out of the use of their data for training models.
- `c5` Entities in DeepSeek's corporate group process data for functions including storage, research and development, and foundation model training.
- `c6` Prighter Group is DeepSeek's privacy representative for the EU.
