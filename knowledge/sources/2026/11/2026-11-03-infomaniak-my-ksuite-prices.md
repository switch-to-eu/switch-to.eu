**Summary:** Infomaniak's price page for my kSuite, read in a browser, which showed euros. It sets out what [[services/euria]] includes on the free and paid personal plans. No date on the page, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` my kSuite+ costs from €1.58 per month, shown as €19.00 annually, against €22.80 when paid monthly ("2 months free").
- `c2` The free my kSuite lists for Euria: 2 projects, 5 conversations per project, 10 shared conversations, 10 favourites and unlimited transcription.
- `c3` Both personal plans list file uploads up to 100 MB and images up to 10 MB.
- `c4` my kSuite+ lists unlimited Euria projects, conversations per project, shared conversations and favourites, "Advanced sovereign AI", unlimited email storage and a choice of 1 or 6 TB of kDrive.
- `c5` The page gives no number of Euria messages for either plan.
