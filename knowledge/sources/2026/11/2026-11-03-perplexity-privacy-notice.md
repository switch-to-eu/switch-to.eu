**Summary:** Perplexity's consumer privacy notice, covering its websites, Comet browser and apps, last updated 2026-10-23. It does not cover Enterprise and API data.

**Claims**
- `c1` Perplexity AI, Inc. is the controller of data processed when people use its services globally.
- `c2` Perplexity processes user content including prompts, queries and uploads.
- `c3` One listed purpose of processing is to improve or create services and products, including its AI models.
- `c4` VeraSafe Ireland Ltd. is its GDPR Article 27 representative in the EU and also its data protection officer.
- `c5` Perplexity says it may process data in countries with less stringent privacy laws than the user's country.
- `c6` It says it does not use the content of emails linked to its Email Assistant to train AI models.
