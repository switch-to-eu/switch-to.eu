**Summary:** Nextcloud's announcement of the KoDaS framework agreement: after a procurement by govdigital eG, IONOS won a four-year framework contract for a collaboration service built on Nextcloud for schools in six German states. IONOS runs it in German data centres. Schools are free to adopt it or not, and other states can join.

**Claims**
- `c1` After a public procurement process run by govdigital eG, IONOS was awarded a four-year framework contract for a cloud-based collaboration solution built on Nextcloud technology.
- `c2` The agreement, called KoDaS, covers Berlin, Bremen, Hamburg, Hesse, North Rhine-Westphalia and Rhineland-Palatinate, and other German states can join.
- `c3` Schools and school authorities can use the service according to their needs, without any obligation to adopt it.
- `c4` The service is IONOS Nextcloud Workspace, which combines file management, online document collaboration and communication tools.
- `c5` IONOS is the main contractor and delivers and operates the service, including the cloud infrastructure in German data centres. Nextcloud provides the open-source collaboration software.
- `c6` Nextcloud argues that students who only know a few big tech products tend to keep using them later, which makes switching harder for organisations.

**Quotes**
> "Schools should teach digital skills, not dependency on individual technology providers." (Frank Karlitschek, CEO of Nextcloud)
