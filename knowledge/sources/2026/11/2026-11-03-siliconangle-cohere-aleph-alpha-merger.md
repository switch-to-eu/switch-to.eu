**Summary:** Trade press report on the signed merger agreement between Toronto-based Cohere and Germany's Aleph Alpha, backed by Schwarz Group.

**Claims**
- `c1` Cohere Inc. and Aleph Alpha GmbH signed a merger agreement on 2026-09-16, formalising a plan announced in April 2026.
- `c2` Schwarz Group planned to provide €500 million in financing.
- `c3` The combined company will reportedly be worth $20 billion.
- `c4` After regulatory clearance the companies intend to operate under the Cohere brand, with dual headquarters in Berlin and Toronto; Aleph Alpha's Heidelberg headquarters becomes a research centre.
- `c5` Aleph Alpha develops small language models for enterprises and government agencies.
