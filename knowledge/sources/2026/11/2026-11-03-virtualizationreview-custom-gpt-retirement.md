**Summary:** Trade press report (David Ramel) on OpenAI's plan to retire custom GPTs in [[services/chatgpt]] and replace them with plugins. Gives the announcement date, which OpenAI's FAQ [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-retirement-faq]] does not.

**Claims**
- `c1` OpenAI announced the retirement of custom GPTs on 2026-09-11 in the ChatGPT release notes on its help site.
- `c2` Custom GPTs are scheduled to retire on 2026-12-11. OpenAI says timing and migration availability may vary by account and workspace.
- `c3` Plugins replace custom GPTs. A plugin can contain skills (reusable instructions and resources), a Model Context Protocol server that connects tools and outside systems, or both. ChatGPT and Codex share a plugin directory.
- `c4` Existing GPT conversations, the selected model, custom actions and sharing settings do not carry over. Migrated plugins start private, and people who used a GPT don't automatically get access to its replacement.
- `c5` OpenAI warns that migrated plugins may respond differently and advises users to retest their workflows.
- `c6` For enterprise customers, creating new custom GPTs ends on 2026-10-26, and customers with an approved deferral can keep using them until 2027-02-11.
