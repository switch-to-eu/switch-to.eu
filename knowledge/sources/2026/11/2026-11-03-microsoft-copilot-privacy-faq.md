**Summary:** Microsoft's privacy FAQ for the consumer Copilot app. A banner says the model-training section applies only to the older Copilot app; a new app version has been available since 2026-08-18 with its own privacy pages. No page date found, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` Microsoft says that, apart from certain categories of users and users who opted out, it uses data from Bing, MSN, Copilot and ad interactions for AI training, including voice and conversation activity with Copilot and uploaded images or files.
- `c2` Excluded from training: users signed in with an organisational Entra ID account, Copilot inside Microsoft 365 Personal or Family, users not signed in, signed-in users under 18, users who opted out, and users in Brazil, China (excluding Hong Kong), Israel, Nigeria, South Korea and Vietnam. The EEA is not on the list.
- `c3` Signed-in users can opt out of the use of their conversations for model training.
- `c4` Personalisation (memory) is on by default where available and can be turned off.
- `c5` If personalised ads are allowed and personalisation is on, Copilot conversation history is used to further personalise ads.
- `c6` Microsoft 365 Copilot conversations (work accounts) are not used to train Copilot's models.
- `c7` An updated Copilot app for web, desktop and mobile is available as of 2026-08-18; the model-training section applies only to the older app.
