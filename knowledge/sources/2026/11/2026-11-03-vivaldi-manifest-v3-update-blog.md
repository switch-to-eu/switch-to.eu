**Summary:** Vivaldi's June 2024 post on Chromium's move from Manifest V2 to V3 extensions and what it means for extensions and the built-in blocker in [[services/vivaldi]]. It gives a plan, not a confirmed later date. Published 2024-06-17; read on 2026-11-03.

**Claims**
- `c1` Chromium is phasing out Manifest V2 extensions. Extensions not updated to Manifest V3 will gradually stop working, and many of them are ad blockers.
- `c2` In June 2024 Vivaldi said it would keep Manifest V2 support as long as Chromium allowed, expected to drop it in June 2025, and said this could happen sooner or later.
- `c3` Vivaldi says its built-in tracker and ad blocker does not depend on Chrome's extension architecture.
- `c4` Vivaldi said version 6.8 added support for the hosts file format to its blocker.
