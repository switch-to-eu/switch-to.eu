**Summary:** Proton's help article on the welcome checklist that raises free [[services/proton-drive]] storage from 2 GB to 5 GB. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` A new free Proton account gets 2 GB of Drive storage, raised to 5 GB after completing a welcome checklist.
- `c2` The checklist must be completed within 30 days of creating the account, and only free accounts are eligible.
- `c3` The three tasks are: upload a file or folder, create a shareable link, and add an account recovery method plus a data recovery method.
- `c4` Any Proton Drive app can be used to complete the upload and link tasks.
- `c5` Proton sends an email confirming the storage increase once the tasks are done.
