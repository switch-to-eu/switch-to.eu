**Summary:** Proton's help article on installing the macOS app of [[services/proton-drive]]. The page shows no date, so the fetch date is used.

**Claims**
- `c1` The macOS app requires macOS 13 or later.
- `c2` The app is downloaded from proton.me/drive/download or from the Computers section of the web app.
- `c3` After sign-in, the Proton Drive folder appears under Locations in Finder (File Provider), and syncing starts after clicking Enable.
- `c4` Files and folders moved, copied or pasted into the Proton Drive folder are synced, and everything in My files is synced to the computer.
