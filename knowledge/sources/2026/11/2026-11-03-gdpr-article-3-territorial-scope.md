**Summary:** The text of the General Data Protection Regulation, used only for Article 3 on territorial scope. Relevant to whether the GDPR applies to non-EU search engines such as [[services/duckduckgo]], [[services/google-search]] and [[services/bing-search]].

**Claims**
- `c1` The GDPR applies to processing in the context of an establishment of a controller or processor in the EU, wherever the processing takes place.
- `c2` The GDPR also applies to controllers and processors not established in the EU when they process personal data of people in the EU in connection with offering them goods or services, paid or free.
- `c3` The GDPR also applies to non-EU controllers and processors that monitor the behaviour of people in the EU.
