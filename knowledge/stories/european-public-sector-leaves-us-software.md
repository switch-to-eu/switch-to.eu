## Summary
Six German states (Berlin, Bremen, Hamburg, Hesse, North Rhine-Westphalia and Rhineland-Palatinate) signed up for a school collaboration platform without software from Microsoft or Google. [[2026-11-03-heise-nextcloud-ionos-school-platform#c1]] IONOS won the four-year KoDaS framework contract through the public IT cooperative govdigital and runs IONOS Nextcloud Workspace in German data centres. Schools are free to use it or not, and other states can join.
