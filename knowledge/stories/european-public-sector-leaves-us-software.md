## Summary
Six German states (Berlin, Bremen, Hamburg, Hesse, North Rhine-Westphalia and Rhineland-Palatinate) signed up for a school collaboration platform without software from Microsoft or Google. [[2026-11-03-heise-nextcloud-ionos-school-platform#c1]] IONOS won the four-year KoDaS framework contract through the public IT cooperative govdigital and runs IONOS Nextcloud Workspace in German data centres. Schools are free to use it or not, and other states can join. [[2026-11-03-nextcloud-six-states-school-cloud#c1]] [[2026-11-03-nextcloud-six-states-school-cloud#c2]] [[2026-11-03-nextcloud-six-states-school-cloud#c3]] [[2026-11-03-nextcloud-six-states-school-cloud#c5]]

In the Netherlands, the Tax Administration, Customs and the Benefits Agency dropped their planned move to Microsoft 365's cloud for an on-premises setup combined with open source, according to a letter to parliament of 2026-10-05. [[2026-11-03-kamerbrief-belastingdienst-m365-on-premises#c1]] Mail and calendar move to on-premises servers in 2027, and European open-source tools for storage and collaboration follow later in 2027 and in 2028. The approach aims to use existing licences as much as possible. [[2026-11-03-kamerbrief-belastingdienst-m365-on-premises#c3]] [[2026-11-03-kamerbrief-belastingdienst-m365-on-premises#c4]]
