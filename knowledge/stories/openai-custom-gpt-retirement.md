## Summary
OpenAI announced on 2026-09-11 that it will retire custom GPTs in [[services/chatgpt]] and replace them with plugins. [[2026-11-03-virtualizationreview-custom-gpt-retirement#c1]] [[2026-11-03-virtualizationreview-custom-gpt-retirement#c3]] Retirement is scheduled for 2026-12-11, when GPTs and their pages become inaccessible. FedRAMP workspaces and Enterprise workspaces with an approved deferral retire on 2027-02-11. OpenAI says timing and migration availability may vary by account and workspace. [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-faq-full-text#c1]] [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-faq-full-text#c2]] [[2026-11-03-virtualizationreview-custom-gpt-retirement#c2]]

Creators migrate from My GPTs. Instructions become a skill, knowledge files become reference files and connected apps become apps in the plugin. [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-faq-full-text#c3]] [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-faq-full-text#c4]] Drafts, the selected model and custom actions do not transfer, and plugins start private. [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-faq-full-text#c5]] [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-faq-full-text#c6]] [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-faq-full-text#c7]] People who only use someone else's GPT do not automatically get its replacement. [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-faq-full-text#c10]] Conversations with GPTs stay accessible after retirement. [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-faq-full-text#c9]]

Outside OpenAI, the nearest equivalents are Skills in [[services/mistral-le-chat]] and Custom Lumos in [[services/proton-lumo]]. [[2026-11-03-mistral-docs-skills#c1]] [[2026-11-03-proton-lumo-2-announcement#c1]]

## Open questions
- Does retirement happen on 2026-12-11 for every personal account? OpenAI says timing may vary.
- OpenAI says migration uses the latest published version, and that publishing is not available on personal accounts. How do personal-account creators move an unpublished draft? [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-faq-full-text#c5]] [[2026-11-03-openai-gpts-help#c2]]
- How many public GPTs will return as public plugins? Making a personal plugin public needs a separate submission. [[2026-11-03-openai-custom-gpt-faq-full-text#c7]]
- Which plugins are limited by region for EU users? OpenAI's help page names region as a factor without detail. [[2026-11-03-openai-plugins-in-chatgpt-help#c2]]
