## Summary
A Hungarian press publisher claimed that Google's Gemini could reproduce one of its articles. On 2026-11-03 Advocate General Maciej Szpunar advised the Court of Justice that AI training on copyrighted works is as a rule allowed under the text-and-data-mining exception, but that chatbots can infringe the press publisher's right through unlawful access or recognisable reuse of an article's style, with the operator liable in some cases. He advised declaring the questions about the case itself inadmissible.
