## Summary
The Commission proposed the Kids Act in September 2026. Politico says it would bar most under-13s from social media and severely restrict access for under-15s, with safety-by-design rules that also reach video games and AI companions. [[2026-11-03-politico-kids-act-national-bans-paused#c4]] [[2026-11-03-politico-kids-act-national-bans-paused#c5]] Several member states kept writing their own bans. On 2026-10-29 the Commission told them to pause overlapping national laws for 12 months, starting with Austria, which objected. Greece's under-15 law is likely to stand because it came first.
