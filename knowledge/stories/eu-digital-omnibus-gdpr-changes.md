## Summary
The Commission proposed the Digital Omnibus on 2025-11-19 to simplify EU digital rules, including the GDPR and the ePrivacy cookie rules. [[2026-11-03-council-digital-omnibus-press-release#c6]] Member states agreed their negotiating mandate on 2026-10-21. It is a position, not law. [[2026-11-03-council-digital-omnibus-mandate#c1]] Parliament's position is expected in February 2027, with trilogues after that. [[2026-11-03-netzpolitik-council-data-omnibus#c2]] The roadmap signed by the three institutions aims to conclude all simplification packages by the end of 2027. [[2026-11-03-council-digital-omnibus-press-release#c7]]

New Article 88 bis says processing for developing and operating AI may be carried out for a legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f). [[2026-11-03-council-digital-omnibus-mandate#c6]] A recital says AI development does not in itself constitute a legitimate interest and is assessed case by case. [[2026-11-03-council-digital-omnibus-mandate#c9]] The Council deleted the Commission's "unconditional right to object" and refers to the existing Article 21 right, which lets a controller continue on compelling legitimate grounds. [[2026-11-03-council-digital-omnibus-mandate#c7]] [[2026-11-03-council-digital-omnibus-mandate#c8]] [[2026-11-03-gdpr-articles-6-and-21-legitimate-interest-objection#c3]] noyb, an advocacy group, reads the text as an automatic presumption of an overriding legitimate interest. [[2026-11-03-noyb-council-omnibus-ai-training#c2]]

On pseudonymised data, the Council text settles the conflict between noyb's and netzpolitik.org's accounts. [[2026-11-03-noyb-council-omnibus-ai-training#c5]] [[2026-11-03-netzpolitik-council-data-omnibus#c4]] The definition of personal data stays. [[2026-11-03-council-digital-omnibus-mandate#c2]] [[2026-11-03-council-digital-omnibus-press-release#c2]] New Article 25a takes pseudonymised data out of the GDPR for third parties unable to identify the person, with limits for controllers, processors and onward transfers. [[2026-11-03-council-digital-omnibus-mandate#c3]] [[2026-11-03-council-digital-omnibus-mandate#c4]]

## Open questions
- What position does Parliament take? netzpolitik.org expects it in February 2027.
- Does the deleted "unconditional right to object" return in the trilogue?
- How often would Article 25a's third-party exception apply in practice? noyb says most IT systems work with pseudonyms. What does the European Data Protection Board's opinion say?
- netzpolitik.org reports Germany voted against, while noyb writes that the Council approved the text "with the support of Germany". The Council document does not record votes. Who is right?
- Does the final law keep the case-by-case wording only in a recital, or move it into the article?
- Does Mistral offer the training opt-out on its Free plan? Its help page offers it to all users, its pricing page lists "Opt-out" only on paid plans. [[2026-11-03-mistral-help-training-opt-out#c1]] [[2026-11-03-mistral-pricing-page#c8]]
