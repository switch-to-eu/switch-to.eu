## Why people are quitting ChatGPT

OpenAI signed a deal with the US Pentagon in early 2025. That was the tipping point for millions of users. ChatGPT uninstall rates jumped 295% in the weeks that followed. The #QuitChatGPT movement grew to over 2.5 million participants.

But the Pentagon deal is only part of the story. OpenAI has faced mounting privacy issues for years:

- **GDPR fines:** Italy's privacy authority fined OpenAI €15 million for GDPR violations
- **Training on your data:** Free and paid consumer plan conversations are used for model training by default
- **No data correction:** OpenAI admits it cannot correct or delete personal data stored in its models, violating core GDPR rights
- **US surveillance:** All consumer data is processed on US servers, subject to US surveillance laws
- **Multiple investigations:** Data protection authorities in Austria, Poland, and other EU countries are investigating OpenAI

Your conversations with ChatGPT reveal how you think, what you work on, and what you care about. That data sits on US servers, under US law, used to train models you have no control over.

## Why European alternatives matter

European AI companies operate under GDPR, the world's strongest privacy framework. That means:

- **Your data stays in Europe.** No transfers to US servers, no exposure to foreign surveillance laws.
- **No training on your conversations.** EU alternatives like Mistral and Proton do not use your chats to improve their models.
- **Real data rights.** You can access, correct, and delete your personal data. These are not optional features — they are legal requirements.
- **Transparent operations.** European companies face stricter accountability and transparency obligations under the EU AI Act.

Switching to a European AI assistant is not just about privacy. It is about supporting an independent European tech ecosystem that puts users first.

## How to cancel your ChatGPT subscription

If you are on a paid ChatGPT plan, here is how to cancel:

1. Open ChatGPT and click your profile icon in the bottom-left corner
2. Go to **Settings** > **Subscription**
3. Click **Manage my subscription**
4. Select **Cancel plan**
5. Confirm your cancellation

Your access continues until the end of your billing period. After that, your account reverts to the free tier.

To delete your data:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Data controls**
2. Turn off **Improve the model for everyone** to stop data training
3. Click **Delete account** if you want to remove everything

Keep in mind: OpenAI may retain some data even after deletion, and cannot remove personal information already embedded in trained models.
