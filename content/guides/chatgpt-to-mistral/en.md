## Why switch from ChatGPT to Mistral Vibe

Mistral Vibe is the AI assistant from Mistral AI, a French company registered in Paris. Until May 2026 the assistant was called Le Chat. The address stayed the same: [chat.mistral.ai](https://chat.mistral.ai).

Mistral says your data is hosted in the EU by default. Its privacy policy names the French data protection authority, CNIL, as a place to complain. Pro costs €14.99 a month before VAT, and the Free plan comes with limited messages and web searches.

Mistral's documentation describes no import from ChatGPT. So you move the useful parts by hand: your custom instructions, what ChatGPT remembers about you, and the instructions behind any GPTs you built. Your old chats stay in the export file from OpenAI.

Worth knowing: Mistral trains on your chats by default too, and its subprocessor list puts web search and image generation with US companies. Step 5 shows how to switch training off.

## Before you start

- **The app is called Vibe now.** In the App Store and Google Play, look for "Vibe by Mistral (ex-Le Chat)".
- **Request your ChatGPT export first.** OpenAI says the download link takes up to 7 days to arrive and expires 24 hours after delivery.
- **Built your own GPTs? Copy them before 11 December 2026.** OpenAI retires custom GPTs on that date, and their pages become inaccessible.
- **Switch off training at Mistral before you paste anything personal** (step 5).

<!-- step-start -->
<!-- step-meta
title: Request your ChatGPT data export
complete: false
-->

On chatgpt.com, open the profile menu and go to **Settings > Data controls**. Under **Export data**, select **Export**, then **Confirm export**. In the iPhone or Android app the path is **Settings > Data controls > Export data**, and the app might send you to the website to finish.

OpenAI sends the download link by email or text message. The ZIP file holds your chat history and account data. While you're in **Data controls**, switch off **Improve the model for everyone**, OpenAI's training setting. The switch covers web and mobile and deletes no chats.

<!-- step-end -->

<!-- step-start -->
<!-- step-meta
title: Copy your custom instructions and memories
complete: false
-->

Open **Settings > Personalization** and copy the **Custom Instructions** field into a text file. In the mobile app, look under **Settings > Customize ChatGPT**.

Then open **Settings > Personalization > Memory** and copy what's saved there. Or ask ChatGPT "What do you remember about me?" and copy the answer.

OpenAI says custom instructions are part of the export. Its help pages don't say the same about saved memories, so copy both now.

<!-- step-end -->

<!-- step-start -->
<!-- step-meta
title: Save your GPTs and project files
complete: false
-->

In a browser, go to [chatgpt.com/gpts](https://chatgpt.com/gpts), open **My GPTs**, pick a GPT and select **Edit GPT**. Copy its name, description and instructions into your text file. OpenAI's help pages don't describe a download for a GPT's knowledge files, so find your original copies of those files. Allow 5 to 10 minutes per GPT.

For each ChatGPT project, open the project's more-options menu, choose **Project settings** and copy the instructions. Download the files you want to keep from the project's sources list.

<!-- step-end -->

<!-- step-start -->
<!-- step-meta
title: Create your Mistral account and install the app
complete: false
-->

Sign up at [chat.mistral.ai](https://chat.mistral.ai). On iPhone or iPad, install **Vibe by Mistral (ex-Le Chat)** from the App Store (iOS 16.4 or later). The Android app has the same name on Google Play.

Start on Free. Pro costs €14.99 a month before VAT and gives up to 6 times the Free message allowance. You'll need a paid plan for Code Interpreter and spreadsheet analysis. Verified students new to Vibe get Pro for €5.99 a month for up to 12 months.

Used the ChatGPT desktop app? Mistral's pricing page lists no Windows or Mac app, so open Vibe in your browser on a computer.

<!-- step-end -->

<!-- step-start -->
<!-- step-meta
title: Switch off model training at Mistral
complete: false
-->

Mistral's help centre says your Vibe chats train its models unless you opt out. On the web, open the [Admin panel](https://admin.mistral.ai), select **Vibe** under **Manage**, and under **Privacy** switch off **Allow your interactions to be used to train our models**. In the iPhone or Android app, go to **Settings > Data & Account Controls** and untick **Enable data sharing**.

Files you upload count as input and follow the same setting. One exception, on every plan: if you rate an answer with thumbs up or down and add a comment, Mistral treats that as permission to use the rating, your message and the answer to improve its models.

<!-- step-end -->

<!-- step-start -->
<!-- step-meta
title: Add your instructions and memories to Vibe
complete: false
-->

In the left sidebar, open **Context > Instructions**, pick a **Tone**, paste your ChatGPT custom instructions and save. They apply to new chats, not to ones you already started.

For memories, start a chat and write "Remember that..." followed by one fact from your list, for example "Remember that I teach maths in Ghent." Check what Vibe saved under **Context > Knowledge**. Health, religion and political views aren't saved unless you switch on **Sensitive data in Knowledge**.

This step takes 10 to 15 minutes for a typical memory list.

<!-- step-end -->

<!-- step-start -->
<!-- step-meta
title: Rebuild your GPTs as Skills and your projects as Projects
complete: false
-->

For each GPT, go to **Context > Skills > New Skill**. Fill in a title, a description of when Vibe should use the Skill, the GPT's instructions as the SKILL.md text, and the GPT's files. Or paste the instructions into a chat, ask Vibe to "turn this into a Skill" and review the draft. Vibe picks a Skill on its own when your request matches the description, or when you name the Skill in your message.

For a ChatGPT project, select **New Project** next to the **Projects** header in the sidebar. Use **Customize** to add the instructions, then upload the files.

<!-- step-end -->

<!-- step-start -->
<!-- step-meta
title: Cancel ChatGPT and delete your OpenAI account
complete: false
-->

Cancel the subscription on the platform you paid through, at least 24 hours before your next billing date. On chatgpt.com, go to **Settings > Billing** and select **Cancel** under **Cancel plan**. App Store and Google Play subscriptions are cancelled in the store. Deleting your OpenAI account doesn't cancel an Apple subscription.

Deleting the account is optional. Open your export ZIP first and check your chats are in there. Then go to **Settings > Account > Delete account**, enter your email address or phone number, type DELETE and select **Permanently delete my account**. In the mobile app, you'll find the option under **Settings > Data controls**.

Deletion is permanent and also ends any OpenAI API access. OpenAI says your data is deleted within 30 days, apart from data OpenAI is required or allowed to keep by law.

## Troubleshooting

### The export email never arrived

Check your spam and promotions folders. OpenAI says to wait up to 7 days before contacting support, and to request a new export if the link expired. Self-service export isn't available in ChatGPT Business or Enterprise workspaces.

### Where are Le Chat, Agents and Memories?

Mistral renamed Le Chat to Vibe in May 2026. In September 2026 Skills replaced Agents and the Knowledge Base replaced Memories, with old memories moved into a topic there. Deep Research became a Skill at the same time.

### Is there an import for my ChatGPT memories?

Mistral announced a "fast import of your memories from ChatGPT" in September 2025. The current Knowledge Base documentation doesn't mention one. Use the "Remember that..." route from step 6.

### Vibe doesn't use a fact I saved

Vibe picks the relevant Knowledge entries at each turn, Mistral says, so not every entry shows up in every chat. Put rules you always want followed in your custom instructions. Entries aren't editable by hand: ask Vibe in a chat to change one.

### The training setting is missing on my Free account

Mistral's help article offers the opt-out to all Vibe users, but its pricing table shows "Opt-out" only next to the paid plans. If you don't see the setting, use **Temporary Chat** for anything personal. Mistral says those chats aren't saved to your history or used for training.

## After the switch

Keep the ChatGPT export somewhere safe. OpenAI's export doesn't restore chats you deleted earlier, and a deleted account stays deleted.

For anything you'd rather not store, start a **Temporary Chat** in Vibe. Mistral says those chats stay out of your history and out of training, and only the fact that a Temporary Chat took place gets logged.

If you leave Mistral later, export your data at [admin.mistral.ai/account/export](https://admin.mistral.ai/account/export) and delete the account in the Admin panel under **Danger Zone**. For prices and trade-offs, see our [Mistral Vibe listing](/services/eu/mistral-le-chat).
