### Will your chats train the models?

Mistral's answer is yes, by default. Vibe uses your input and output for training until you opt out in settings. Enterprise accounts start opted out. Temporary Chat isn't saved or used for training, which helps with a one-off personal question.

Infomaniak says Euria never trains on your data, files or chats. Proton says the same for Lumo, apart from feedback you choose to send on an Apertus 1.5 answer. Both are the companies' own statements.

Ecosia says it doesn't train models at all, and that Mistral keeps nothing from Ecosia's chats. Neither page says whether Mistral trains on them. OpenAI is Ecosia's backup when Mistral is unavailable, and OpenAI keeps chats for 30 days.

### Where is the company, and where do your chats go?

Two companies here have a head office in the EU: Mistral in Paris and Ecosia in Berlin. Infomaniak and Proton are Swiss, both from Geneva.

Mistral says data is hosted in the EU by default, but its provider list puts web search (Brave) and image generation (Black Forest Labs) in the US. Infomaniak says all Euria data stays in Switzerland, on its own servers. Proton says Lumo runs on servers it controls in Europe, and doesn't name the countries. Ecosia keeps your chat list in your browser, and on its servers if you turn on chat history. It also keeps uploaded files for 7 days and a sample of conversations, anonymised according to Ecosia, for up to 30.

### Which models answer you?

Mistral runs its own models. Proton names what Lumo uses for text: Qwen 3.5, GLM 5.3 and Apertus 1.5. Infomaniak says Euria runs open source models and doesn't say which one answers a chat. Its guide lists Mistral, Whisper, DeepSeek, Llama and Granite as the base of its AI. Ecosia AI Chat is a front end, not a separate model: your chat goes mainly to Mistral, and Ecosia says it also uses open-weight models.

### How much will you use the assistant?

Mistral, Proton and Infomaniak all limit free messages, and none of them publishes the number. Euria's heavier use comes with my kSuite+ at €19 a year before VAT, which also includes 1 TB of storage. Lumo Plus costs €9.99 a month billed yearly, or €12.99 billed monthly. Mistral Pro costs €14.99 a month before VAT, or €18.14 with Dutch VAT, and gives up to 6 times the free messages. Ecosia sells no plan in Europe: its subscription is a US test.

### What else do you need?

For images, Mistral and Lumo both make and edit them, and Ecosia makes them if you have an account. Mistral lists more than 100 connectors to other tools, including email, calendar and Slack. Euria has unlimited transcription of audio and sits inside Infomaniak's Mail and kDrive. On my kSuite, Euria in Mail needs the paid my kSuite+ plan. Lumo links to your files in Proton Drive.

Ecosia AI Chat has one catch before all of this. It's a beta for some users only, so you might not get the chat at all.
