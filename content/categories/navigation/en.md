# Workflow
updatedAt: '2026-03-30'
---

Navigation apps know more about us than almost any other tool we use daily - where we live, where we work, our routines, and everywhere we go. European navigation alternatives prioritize keeping this sensitive location data private and under your control.

EU-based and privacy-focused navigation apps offer reliable turn-by-turn directions, place search, and offline maps without the constant tracking and data harvesting found in mainstream alternatives. Many are built on OpenStreetMap, a community-driven mapping project that ensures map data remains open and independent from Big Tech.
