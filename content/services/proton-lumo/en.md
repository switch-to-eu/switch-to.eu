Proton Lumo is the AI assistant from Proton AG, the Swiss company behind Proton Mail. Proton says Lumo runs on open-source models on servers it controls in Europe, so no outside AI company sees your prompts. Proton names the models: Qwen 3.5, GLM 5.3 and Apertus 1.5 for text, and Image-Turbo and FireRed-Image-Edit-1.1 for images.

Saved chats get zero-access encryption, and Proton says that leaves the company no way to read them. Proton also says it keeps no logs, and that your query is erased once Lumo answers. If you don't want a chat saved at all, Ghost mode makes the chat disappear when you close the window. You don't need a Proton account to start. Guest chats are erased at the end of the session.

Lumo answers questions, summarises documents, works on code and creates and edits images. Web search is there when you want recent information, and stays off until you switch it on. Projects keep chats, files and instructions together, and Custom Lumos save your instructions for a repeated task. You link files from Proton Drive and dictate on the web and in the iOS and Android apps.

The free plan limits messages, chat history, image generations and use of the Max model, with 1 Project. Lumo Plus costs €9.99 a month billed yearly (€119.88), or €12.99 billed monthly. You get more of each, plus unlimited Projects and Custom Lumos.

## Worth knowing

The no-logs and no-training promises are Proton's own statements. The one exception Proton names: feedback you choose to send on an Apertus 1.5 answer goes anonymised to Proton and the Apertus research teams. Zero-access encryption covers your saved history. While Lumo works on a prompt, Proton's servers read the text, and Proton's security post says this isn't end-to-end encryption.

Proton doesn't publish the free plan's limits, and doesn't name its web search engines or the countries its Lumo servers are in. Proton says Lumo's code is open source, but the pages we read don't say whether that includes the server code. Proton's head office is in Switzerland, outside the EU.
