  ## Company Background

  HERE Technologies is one of the world's largest location data companies, based in the Netherlands. HERE's own pages don't name a headquarters city (as of November 2026): HERE Global B.V. (the data controller) and HERE Europe B.V. (the app publisher) are registered at Kennedyplein 222-226 in Eindhoven, and HERE's press releases are datelined Amsterdam. The company traces back to 1985 (originally Karlin & Collins, later NAVTEQ). Nokia acquired the mapping business and developed Nokia Maps/HERE Maps. In 2015, a consortium of Audi, BMW, and Daimler (now Mercedes-Benz Group) acquired HERE for 2.8 billion EUR. In June 2020, after Mitsubishi Corporation and NTT jointly bought a 30% stake, HERE listed nine direct and indirect shareholders: Audi, Bosch, BMW Group, Continental, Intel Capital, Mitsubishi Corporation, Mercedes-Benz, NTT and Pioneer. No newer ownership statement found on here.com (as of November 2026). The company has 6,500+ employees and 40 years of mapping heritage.

  ## Product Details

  HERE WeGo is a free consumer navigation app available on iOS, Android, and web. Key features: turn-by-turn navigation (driving, walking, cycling), public transit in 1,900+ cities, offline maps by region, country or continent (no country count on HERE's pages, and the help page names Japan, Korea and China as exceptions, as of November 2026), multi-modal trip planning, traffic-aware routing, POI search, indoor venue maps for 100,000+ buildings in 87 countries. In February 2025, HERE launched WeGo Pro for truck navigation. The app has been downloaded 41 million times as of 2026.

  ## Privacy and GDPR

  Data controller is HERE Global B.V. in Eindhoven, Netherlands. The WeGo privacy supplement (read November 2026) says: location data is used only with consent and linked to random identifiers that don't identify you; nothing is sent to HERE when the app is used only offline; traffic data carries an identifier that persists for the duration of one route; activity records are kept for a short period before being anonymized. It does not say location data is deleted when a navigation session ends (the earlier listing said so). The HERE support FAQ says no personal data is collected without explicit consent, but the supplement also lists device and technical data (device ID, IP address) collected automatically. The supplement and HERE's privacy policy allow location-based and behaviourally profiled advertising in the app and sharing of non-personal, aggregated data with advertisers, so "not from advertising" is not supported. HERE's About page names its customers as automakers, logistics firms, mobility providers and technology brands. Data may transfer outside the EEA (the policy names the US as an example) under EU Standard Contractual Clauses, and HERE says it uses servers in various countries. Users have rights of access, correction, deletion and portability.

  ## User Sentiment

  Mixed reviews. Praised for: clean interface, accurate navigation, excellent offline maps, good transit coverage, lightweight app. Criticized for: recent UI updates that reduced map visibility during navigation, low contrast in day/night modes, occasional map data disappearing after updates. Overall considered a solid Google Maps alternative with strong privacy credentials.

HERE WeGo gives you turn-by-turn navigation, public transit directions and downloadable offline maps in one free app. It comes from HERE Technologies, a Dutch mapping company that sells map data to carmakers and logistics firms. Its shareholders include Audi, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Intel, Mitsubishi Corporation and NTT.

The offline maps are the standout feature. Download a region, country or continent before you travel, then navigate without mobile data and skip roaming charges. Public transit routing covers more than 1,900 cities.

Plan trips by car, bike, public transport or on foot, with extra stops if you need them. Arrival times take live traffic into account. The app runs on iOS, Android and the web at wego.here.com.

On privacy: HERE's privacy notice for the app says it links your location data to random identifiers, not to you. If you use the app only offline, it sends no location data to HERE. Sharing traffic data is your choice. HERE Global B.V. in Eindhoven controls your data, so GDPR applies.

## Worth knowing

Offline maps aren't available everywhere. HERE's help pages name Japan, Korea and China as exceptions. Before you go offline, switch on "Use app offline" in the menu. Business listings come from HERE's own map data, so search for a few places you visit often before you switch. HERE WeGo Pro is a separate truck navigation app for business fleets, not a paid version of this one.
