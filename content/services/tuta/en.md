  ## Company Overview

  Tuta (formerly Tutanota, rebranded November 2023) is an encrypted email, calendar and contacts service operated by Tutao GmbH, based in Hanover, Germany. Founded in 2011 by Arne Möhle and Matthias Pfau, who remain the sole owners. The company has approximately 41 employees. Tuta serves over 10 million users including financial institutions and human rights organizations. The company is self-funded with no venture capital.

  ## Pricing

  Free tier: 1 GB storage, 1 calendar, 3 labels, standard addresses only. Revolutionary: €3/month (yearly), 20 GB, unlimited calendars/labels, 15 extra addresses, 3 custom domains. Legend: €8/month (yearly), 500 GB, 30 extra addresses, 10 custom domains, priority support. Add-ons available for extra storage (€1/GB/month).

  ## Privacy and GDPR

  Fully GDPR compliant. All data stored on own servers in ISO 27001-certified data centers in Germany. DPA available. Privacy by design: does not log IP addresses by default. End-to-end encryption covers email content, subject lines, contacts, and calendar entries. Metadata (sender/recipient addresses) not encrypted due to email protocol requirements. Tuta uses its own encryption protocol rather than PGP, which means encrypted emails can only be sent between Tuta users or via password-protected messages.

  ## Security and Certifications

  Data centers are ISO 27001 certified and powered by 100% renewable energy. Tuta is developing quantum-safe encryption (TutaCrypt) to protect against future quantum computing threats. All client apps are open source under GPLv3 on GitHub. One known vulnerability disclosure: in 2022, Tutanota apps version 3.112.5 had an HTML attribute injection vulnerability, discovered by a user and patched quickly. No known data breaches involving user data.

  ## Open Source

  All Tuta client apps (web, Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS) are open source under GPLv3. Available on GitHub at github.com/tutao/tutanota and on F-Droid. Server-side code is not open source. Note: there has been some community discussion about whether Tuta's license truly qualifies as open source due to certain restrictions, but the code is publicly available and auditable.

Tuta is an encrypted email service from Tutao GmbH, a founder-owned company in Hanover, Germany with about 41 employees. It encrypts your emails, contacts, and calendar so that nobody can read them. Not the company, not anyone else. Over 10 million people use it, including financial institutions and human rights organizations.

The encryption goes further than most competitors. Tuta encrypts subject lines too, which other secure email providers typically leave readable. It also skips IP logging by default, so the company holds very little data about you even if someone comes asking. All Tuta apps are open source and the code is public for anyone to check.

Beyond email, Tuta includes an encrypted calendar and contacts manager. You can send encrypted messages to people who don't use Tuta by setting a shared password. The service runs on servers in Germany powered by renewable energy.

## Worth knowing

Tuta uses its own encryption protocol instead of PGP. That means you can only send encrypted emails to other Tuta users or through password-protected messages. Search is slower than you'd expect because everything has to be decrypted on your device first. The free plan gives you 1 GB of storage, which fills up quickly with attachments. Some users find the interface less polished than Proton Mail, and there's no way to insert links directly in the email composer.
