  ## Privacy and Data Collection

  Waze collects extensive location and driving behavior data. The March 2026 privacy policy update expanded collection to include contact phone numbers (via a 'find friends' feature) and social network account connections. Data is shared with Google and advertising partners for personalized ad delivery. Waze displays hyperlocal ads during navigation. Location tracking continues in the background even when the app is not actively in use (for planned drive reminders and parking assistance). An 'Invisible Mode' exists but only hides your icon from other Waze users on the map, it does not stop data collection by Waze/Google.

  ## Security Incidents

  In February 2025, a threat actor claimed to be selling 7.7 million Waze user records including usernames, unique IDs, and GPS locations. In 2019, researcher Peter Gasper found vulnerabilities in Waze Live Map that allowed tracking individual users over time via unchanging ID numbers (patched after bug bounty). In 2016, UC Santa Barbara researchers demonstrated fake traffic injection and user tracking via weak location authentication.

  ## Corporate Changes

  Google merged Waze and Maps teams in December 2022 into the Geo division. Waze CEO Neha Parikh left and was not replaced. Layoffs followed in June 2023 as Waze ads were transitioned to Google Ads. Waze Carpool was shut down in August 2022. The app continues as a separate product but shares infrastructure and advertising systems with Google Maps.

Waze is a free navigation app built around crowdsourced traffic data. Millions of drivers report accidents, road closures, speed traps, and hazards in real time, and the app uses that data to reroute you around problems. Google bought Waze in 2013 for $1.3 billion, and in 2022 folded the team into its Geo division alongside Google Maps.

The app's biggest draw is its community. Reports from other drivers give you a heads-up about police, potholes, objects on the road, and stopped traffic before you reach them. Waze also compares fuel prices at nearby stations and integrates with Spotify and other audio apps so you can control music without leaving the navigation screen.

The trade-off for all of this is data. Waze needs a constant internet connection and GPS signal to work. There is no offline mode. Every drive you take is logged: your route, your speed, your braking patterns, your frequent destinations. That data goes to Google, where it feeds into ad targeting across Google's products. Waze itself shows location-based ads while you drive, promoting nearby businesses at stoplights and along your route.

## Worth knowing

Waze updated its privacy policy in March 2026 to collect contact phone numbers through a "find friends" feature and to link social network accounts. Background location tracking runs even when you are not actively navigating, for features like planned drive reminders and parking assistance. The app has an "Invisible Mode," but that only hides your avatar from other users on the map. It does not stop Waze or Google from collecting your location data. In early 2025, a threat actor claimed to have stolen 7.7 million Waze user records including GPS coordinates. Waze Carpool was shut down in 2022, and the standalone Waze ad system was merged into Google Ads in 2023.
