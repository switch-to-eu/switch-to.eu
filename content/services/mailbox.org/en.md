mailbox, called mailbox.org until its 2025 relaunch, is a German email provider based in Berlin. The Heinlein Group, a German Linux and email specialist with over 30 years of experience, runs the service. mailbox says all its servers are in data centres in Berlin, so your data falls under German data protection law and the GDPR.

The service goes beyond email. Even the €1 Light plan includes a calendar and address book. Standard adds an online office suite, 10 GB of cloud storage, web chat and the option to use your own domain.

Inbox encryption is optional. Switch it on and mailbox locks new incoming emails with your PGP key. Its help pages say only someone with that key and its password can then open them. PGP encryption also works right in the browser.

mailbox holds ISO 27001 certification and a C5 attestation from BSI, Germany's federal IT security office. In 2026, German consumer testers Stiftung Warentest gave the Light plan top marks for privacy, according to mailbox.

## Worth knowing

There's no free plan. You get a 30-day trial, then it costs at least €1 a month, paid a year at a time. Paying monthly costs more: Standard is €4 instead of €3, and Premium €12 instead of €9.

Support depends on the plan. Light covers questions about your contract, credit and password only. mailbox says Standard users usually get a reply within 48 hours on business days, and Premium users within 24 hours.
