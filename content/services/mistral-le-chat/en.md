Mistral Vibe is the AI assistant from Mistral AI, a Paris company founded in 2023. It used to be called Le Chat. The address is still chat.mistral.ai, and your account, plan and conversations moved across with the new name. Vibe answers questions, writes and rewrites text, searches the web, reads your documents and makes images. You use Vibe in the browser or in the iOS and Android apps, with voice input on every plan. There's also a Code mode for developers.

Mistral says your data is hosted in the EU by default. Two features leave: its provider list puts web search (Brave) and image generation (Black Forest Labs) in the US.

The Free plan limits your messages and web searches, and Mistral doesn't publish the numbers. Pro costs €14.99 a month before VAT, which is €18.14 with Dutch VAT. You get up to 6 times the messages, 5 times the web searches and 40 times the image generations. Verified students new to Vibe pay €5.99 before VAT, for at most 12 months.

The Deep Research skill writes longer reports, and connectors link Vibe to more than 100 tools, including email, calendar and Slack.

Mistral also publishes some models as open-weight downloads, so you run them on your own servers if you want to. The licences set conditions on commercial use.

## Worth knowing

Mistral trains its models on your Vibe chats by default. You opt out at any time: on the web in the Admin panel at admin.mistral.ai, under Vibe and Privacy, or in the apps under Settings, Data & Account Controls. Uploaded documents fall under the same setting. Mistral's help page offers this to all Vibe users, though its pricing table shows "Opt-out" only next to the paid plans. Enterprise accounts start opted out. Temporary Chat isn't saved or used for training.

A thumbs up or down with a comment gives Mistral permission to use that chat for training, whatever your setting. Mistral keeps your chats until you delete them or your account, and zero data retention isn't available in Vibe on any plan.

In February 2025, a lawyer filed a complaint with CNIL, France's data protection authority, because free users had no opt-out at the time. A search on 3 November 2026 found no public decision. Code Interpreter and spreadsheet analysis need a paid plan.
