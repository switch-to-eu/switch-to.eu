On 3 September 2025 the EU General Court dismissed an action by French MP Philippe Latombe against the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The framework is the legal basis that lets companies send personal data from the EU to certified US companies without extra safeguards.

The court found that the United States provided an adequate level of protection at the time the Commission adopted the framework in 2023. It pointed to the US Data Protection Review Court, set up to handle complaints from Europeans, as a meaningful redress mechanism.

## Third time lucky?

This is the third attempt at an EU-US data deal. The first two, Safe Harbor and Privacy Shield, were struck down by the Court of Justice in 2015 and 2020 after challenges by privacy activist Max Schrems. The new ruling comes from the General Court, the lower of the EU's two courts. An appeal to the Court of Justice can still be brought, and privacy groups have signalled that the question isn't settled.

Worth knowing: the framework rests partly on a US executive order, which a future US administration can change without Congress.

## What this means for you
