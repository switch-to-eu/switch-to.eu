# Design audit: homepage, news and service pages

**Date:** 2026-11-02
**Method:** impeccable `audit` (`.claude/skills/impeccable/reference/audit.md`), run by hand. The bundled detector ships as a downloaded binary, which this environment did not allow to run, so the measurable checks were reimplemented as a Playwright script: `apps/website/scripts/a11y-audit.cjs`. It measures rendered pages, not source.
**Pages:** `/en`, `/nl`, `/en/news`, an article, a category page, an EU and a non-EU service page, a guide, `/en/feedback`, `/en/about`, a landing page. Each at 1280px and 375px.

## Findings and status

### P1 (WCAG AA violations): fixed

- **Palette pairs below 4.5:1 for body text.** White on orange 3.79, green on pink 4.03, navy on yellow 4.29, yellow on navy 4.29. Fix: orange and pink cards take `#1a1a1a` text (4.59 and 6.7), yellow buttons and pills take brand green (5.27), yellow outline buttons on dark grounds take white text. Files: `packages/ui/src/lib/brand-palette.ts`, `tool-colors.ts`, `NewsletterCta`, `ServiceCta`, `RecommendedAlternative`, `SidebarAlternative`, the EU and non-EU service pages, the news pages.
- **Faded text.** `opacity-60/70/80` and `text-*/30..55` on card copy, footer, tabs, the affiliate disclosure and form notes fell to 1.9 to 4.4. Fix: full-strength colours, or at least 80% where measured to pass. Hierarchy now comes from size and weight.
- **EU region badge** (3.22): new `.badge-eu` in brand green (10.5).
- **Non-EU hero** on orange with 90% white text: moved to brand red with full white (4.7). The pills on it use a darker ground.
- **Feedback banner** on pink with white text (2.6): `#1a1a1a` text (6.7), per brand.md's light-colour rule.
- **Affiliate disclosure on dark grounds:** its class override lost to the default grey (1.1 to 1.4). Now merged with `cn()`.

### P2: fixed

- **Touch targets under 24px** (WCAG 2.5.8): category links on pick cards, "All news", "RSS feed", "← All news" and the affiliate disclosure. Fix: vertical padding.
- **Heading skip** h1→h3 on `/news`: the front page uses h2s when it has no section title.
- **9px "Recommended" badge text:** raised to 11px and 12px.

### False positives (verified by hand)

- **"Recommended by Switch-to.eu" (1.00):** green text on a yellow SVG shape (5.27). The script can't see SVG fills.
- **Unlabelled select on `/feedback`:** Radix's hidden native `<select aria-hidden>`. The visible trigger is labelled by `FormLabel`.

### Not covered

- Focus order and keyboard traps were not tested automatically. Focus rings exist on the new components (`focus-visible:ring-*`).
- Gesture and touch interactions weren't synthesised.
- Performance wasn't profiled.
